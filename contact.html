Get in touch

Questions about an article, a correction to a date, an idea for a future guide, or a partnership enquiry — we read every message.

Email us

The fastest way to reach EventBuzz is by email. Please include the article name or URL if your message is about a specific guide.

Before you write

Quick answers

Can I use your wishes or quotes?

Yes — our wishes, messages and short quotes are written to be copied and shared. A link back to EventBuzz is appreciated but not required for personal use.

Can I suggest an event?

Absolutely. Tell us the event name, date (if known) and why it matters to your community. Reader suggestions shape our calendar.

Do you accept guest posts?

We are not accepting guest posts at launch. For collaborations, please email us with “Partnership” in the subject line.
