## 1. سوال: When is Black Friday 2026?
جواب:
Black Friday 2026 falls on **November 27, 2026** — the day after Thanksgiving (November 26).

A few practical tips from years of tracking it:
- Start your wishlist early and track prices now, so you can spot real discounts vs inflated "was" prices.
- Big-ticket electronics (TVs, laptops) see the deepest cuts on Black Friday itself; smaller accessories often drop further on Cyber Monday (November 30).
- Check store ads the week before Thanksgiving — many deals go live early now.

## 4. سوال: When is Cyber Monday 2026?
جواب:
Cyber Monday 2026 is on **November 30, 2026** — the Monday after Thanksgiving weekend.

Quick strategy:
- Cyber Monday is strongest for online-only deals: tech accessories, small gadgets, fashion.
- Big TVs and appliances are usually cheaper on Black Friday itself.
- Stack coupon codes on top of sale prices — retailers allow it more often on Cyber Monday.

## 6. سوال: What is Friendsgiving and how do I host one?
جواب:
Friendsgiving is Thanksgiving celebrated with friends — usually the weekend before or after the holiday.

Hosting basics:
- **Potluck** — assign dishes so you're not cooking everything.
- **One signature cocktail or mocktail** — keeps drinks simple.
- **Decor** — a simple fall table runner, mini pumpkins, and candles go a long way.
- **Games** — card games after dinner keep the night going.
