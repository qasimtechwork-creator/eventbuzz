01About Us

We help you celebrate better

EventBuzz started with a simple frustration: event information online is scattered, outdated or buried in clutter. We wanted one calm, friendly place for dates, background and ideas you can trust.

What EventBuzz is

EventBuzz is an event blogging website covering four everyday categories: festivals, holidays, sports events and shopping events. For each event we publish clear, original guides — when it happens, why it matters, how people celebrate, and practical ideas for wishes, costumes, parties, food, viewing and shopping.

Our launch series focuses on Halloween 2026, with five connected guides so families, teachers, hosts and first-time celebrants can plan the whole season from one place.

Our mission

To make every celebration easier to understand and more joyful to share. That means:

  • Correct dates, first. Every guide leads with the verified date and day of the week.
  • Original, human writing. We explain traditions in our own words and create wishes and ideas you can genuinely use.
  • Practical over promotional. Checklists, timelines and copy-ready text come before hype.
  • Respect for every culture. Festivals and holidays are covered with curiosity and care, not stereotypes.

How we create content

Each EventBuzz article is researched, written and edited as a standalone guide. We check calendars and widely reported facts, write in a warm and accessible tone, and structure every piece with headings, FAQs and related reading so you can skim or dive deep. We do not copy content from other websites, and we update seasonal guides when dates or details change.

Where we offer wishes, quotes, costume ideas, party games or recipes, our goal is usefulness: short options for messages, longer options for cards and speeches, and variations for kids, families, friends and workplaces.

Who EventBuzz is for

  • Families planning weekends, school events and holidays
  • Teachers and community organisers who need clear explainers
  • Hosts looking for party, food and decoration ideas
  • Fans who want sports schedules and viewing inspiration
  • Smart shoppers tracking major sale events and seasonal offers
  • Anyone who simply loves a good celebration

What is coming next

After Halloween, we will expand into Diwali, Christmas, New Year, Eid, Thanksgiving, major sports finals, Black Friday, 11.11 and more. Our latest articles and category pages are the best place to follow new guides as they are published.

Work with us / say hello

Questions, corrections or partnership ideas are welcome. Visit our Contact page — we read every message and aim to reply within a few working days.

EventBuzz is an independent informational website. Event dates are provided for general guidance; please confirm local observances, school schedules and official event timings with organisers where it matters.
