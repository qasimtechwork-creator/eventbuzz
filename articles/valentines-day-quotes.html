Valentine's Day Quotes: Romantic, Short and Sweet Sayings

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

A good quote does the heavy lifting when your own words feel shy. This page brings together timeless classic lines and original short sayings written fresh for Valentine's Day 2027 — made for cards, captions, gift tags and the text you send at midnight.

Quick answer: Valentine's Day 2027 falls on Sunday, February 14, 2027. For a card, pair one classic line with one original line of your own; for a caption, the short sayings further down this page were written exactly for that.

Timeless Classic Lines

A few brief, famous lines from writers whose words now belong to everyone. Keep them short, credit the author, and let your own sentence do the rest.

"If music be the food of love, play on." — William Shakespeare
"How do I love thee? Let me count the ways." — Elizabeth Barrett Browning
"Life is the flower for which love is the honey." — Victor Hugo
"Love does not consist in gazing at each other, but in looking outward together in the same direction." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Original Romantic Quotes for Cards

Written fresh for 2027. Choose the one that sounds most like the two of you.

Love is not the days we count. It is the moments we remember without trying.
You are the quiet answer to every loud worry I have ever had.
Whatever forever is made of, I want mine built beside you.
Some people search their whole lives for a feeling I get from your hello.
My favourite place in the world is wherever your hand finds mine.
Loving you was never a decision. It was a recognition.

Short & Sweet Sayings

One line each — for gift tags, card corners and short texts.

  • You, me, and every ordinary day made extraordinary. Happy Valentine's Day.
  • Still my favourite person to tell everything to. Be mine.
  • Every season of my life got better the day you arrived in it.
  • Home is not an address. It is your laugh from the next room.
  • I would find you in every lifetime and choose you in every one.
  • You make my heart behave embarrassingly well in public.
  • Of all the love stories in the world, thank you for writing ours with me.
  • Today is for saying it out loud: I adore you.

Caption-Ready Lines for Photos

Short enough for a caption, warm enough for the photo that goes with it.

  • Found my person. Keeping them. Happy Valentine's Day.
  • Love looks good on us.
  • February 14: the official excuse to post this face.
  • Still crushing on you, years later.
  • My favourite hello and my hardest goodbye.
  • Proof that the best plans are the unplanned ones.

How to Use a Quote Well

Three small rules make any quote land. First, pick the line that matches a real memory, not just the prettiest words. Second, add one specific sentence of your own after it — a place, a date, a small kindness only the two of you would recognise. Third, keep the card short; one classic line, one original line and your name beats a page of verses every time. Planning the whole day around that card? Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good short Valentine's Day quote for a card?

"Love is not the days we count. It is the moments we remember without trying." Follow it with one personal sentence and their name, and the card is complete.

Are the classic quotes on this page free to use?

Yes. The classic lines quoted here are brief, famous excerpts from authors like Shakespeare, Elizabeth Barrett Browning, Victor Hugo and Antoine de Saint-Exupéry, whose works are in the public domain. Credit the author when you use them.

What should I write on Instagram as a Valentine's caption?

Keep it short and specific: "Found my person. Keeping them." or "Still crushing on you, years later." Add one shared detail in the comments if you want it to feel personal.

Can I use the original sayings on this page in my own card?

Yes. Every original saying here was written by EventBuzz and is free for personal use in cards, messages, captions and gift tags.
