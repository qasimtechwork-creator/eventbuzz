Mother’s Day 2027: Date, History & Ways to Celebrate

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

She remembered every birthday of yours for your whole life. Mother’s Day is the one day a year the calendar asks you to return the favour, not with panic buying, but with thanks. This guide gives you the exact Mother’s Day 2027 date, a short history of how the day began, how the date differs around the world, and warm ways to celebrate whether you live in the same house or across an ocean.

Quick answer: Mother’s Day 2027 falls on Sunday, 9 May 2027, the second Sunday of May. Mark it now, order flowers or a card a week early, and plan one personal gesture she will remember.

Mother’s Day 2027 Date

Mother’s Day in the United States, Pakistan, India, Canada, Australia and many other countries is celebrated on the second Sunday of May. In 2027, that is Sunday, 9 May 2027. The date moves every year, which is why people get caught out; put it in your calendar today with a reminder one week before. That early reminder is when you order flowers, book the restaurant before the rush, or post the card that must travel. Florists and bakeries fill quickly in Mother’s Day week, so early planners get the best slots at normal prices.

A Short History of Mother’s Day

Honouring mothers is ancient, but the modern Mother’s Day has a clear beginning. In 1908, Anna Jarvis held a memorial for her mother in Grafton, West Virginia, and campaigned for a national day honouring the sacrifices mothers make. Her symbol was the white carnation, her mother’s favourite flower. The campaign grew until, in 1914, the day was officially recognised as a national day in the United States, fixed on the second Sunday of May. From there it travelled the world, adapting to local customs. Jarvis later worried the day was becoming too commercial, and her original intention is worth keeping: a handwritten note, a spoken thank you, and time given with a whole heart.

How Mother’s Day Dates Differ by Country

If your family is spread across countries, one May date may not cover everyone. The second Sunday of May, Sunday 9 May 2027, is used by the United States, Pakistan, India, Canada, Australia, South Africa, the Philippines and many other countries. The United Kingdom and Ireland celebrate Mothering Sunday on the fourth Sunday of Lent, which falls in March. France usually marks its Fête des Mères on the last Sunday of May, Spain on the first Sunday of May, and in parts of the Middle East Mother’s Day is commonly observed on 21 March. For international families, celebrate on the date of the country where your mother lives, and treat any second date as a happy bonus. She will not mind being loved twice.

Ways to Celebrate at Home

If you share a home or a city with your mother, the best celebrations are built from attention, not expense. Give her a slow morning with no responsibilities: her favourite breakfast, no kitchen duties, and the errands she usually carries silently handled by you. Cook the meal she loves, gather the family around one table without phones, and let conversation wander through old stories. Ask about her childhood and her funniest memory of you, and listen like it matters, because one day those stories become family treasure. Gifts should lean personal over pricey: a framed photograph, her favourite book with a note inside, or a handwritten letter naming specific things she did and what they built in you.

Ways to Celebrate from Far Away

Distance changes the method, never the meaning. Anchor the day with a real conversation: agree the time in both time zones a few days before Sunday 9 May 2027, and give her your full, unhurried attention on video, because seeing your face is the gift itself. Layer small arrivals around the call. Order flowers or sweets from a local shop near her home for morning delivery. Post a handwritten card ten to fourteen days early, and send a recorded voice note or short video she can replay. If family is scattered across countries, one shared group call, even twenty loud happy minutes, will carry her for weeks. What she remembers is that you planned and remembered.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Mother’s Day 2027?

Mother’s Day 2027 falls on Sunday, 9 May 2027, the second Sunday of May, in the United States, Pakistan, India, Canada, Australia and many other countries.

Who started Mother’s Day?

The modern celebration began with Anna Jarvis in the United States, who held a memorial for her mother in 1908 and campaigned for a national day. It became an official United States national day in 1914.

Why does the UK have a different Mother’s Day date?

The United Kingdom and Ireland celebrate Mothering Sunday on the fourth Sunday of Lent, a tradition with older church roots. In 2027 that date falls in March, separate from the May celebration.

What is the best gift for Mother’s Day?

The gifts mothers treasure most are personal: a handwritten letter, a framed photo, her favourite meal shared slowly, or planned time together. Flowers plus one personal gesture is a combination that rarely fails.

How early should I order flowers or send a card?

Order flowers about a week before Sunday 9 May 2027 for the best choice and prices. Post international cards ten to fourteen days ahead, or use a local florist near your mother for reliable same week delivery.

Conclusion

Mother’s Day 2027, on Sunday 9 May, is a whole year of gratitude given one beautiful date. Mark it today, plan one personal gesture, and let your mother hear, in words she can keep, how much of who you are began with her.
