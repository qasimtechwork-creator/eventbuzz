Best Buy Black Friday Deals 2026: Tech Discounts Worth Grabbing

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

If you are buying tech on Black Friday, November 27, 2026, Best Buy deserves the first look. The retailer anchors its holiday season on deep electronics discounts, and its Black Friday ad historically sets the market price for TVs, laptops, and headphones. Best Buy also matches its own online prices in store, offers free next-day delivery on many items, and backs purchases with an extended holiday return window. Here is where the real deals are, and how to make sure you actually get them.

TV Deals: The Main Event

Televisions are Best Buy's signature Black Friday category, and the discounts are aggressive. Expect entry-level 55-inch 4K TVs from TCL and Hisense around $230 to $280, midrange 65-inch QLED sets under $500, and premium 55-inch OLED models marked down $300 to $500 from their fall prices. Big-screen doorbusters, like 75-inch models under $600, tend to appear in limited quantities and sell out fast both online and in store.

Best Buy's open-box and clearance TVs can stack even further: open-box excellent-condition sets often carry an additional 10 to 20 percent off the sale price. If you are flexible on brand, the savings are enormous; if you want a specific OLED model, decide early, because the popular sizes rarely last past Thanksgiving weekend. Look for bundle offers that throw in a free wall mount, streaming device, or soundbar.

Laptop and Computer Deals

Best Buy's laptop deals cover the full range. Chromebooks routinely drop to $130 to $180, making them the cheapest credible gift laptops of the season. Midrange Windows laptops with 16GB of RAM and 512GB SSDs fall into the $450 to $600 range, down from $700 to $900. MacBook Air models typically see $150 to $250 off, which is as good as Apple discounts get outside of education pricing.

Gaming laptops see some of the steepest cuts, often $200 to $400 off last season's RTX models as retailers clear inventory for new releases. Desktops, monitors, and printers follow the same pattern: 25 to 40 percent off. If you are buying for a student, check whether Best Buy's student deals portal adds an extra coupon on top of the sale price.

Headphones, Audio and Wearables

Audio is one of the most reliably discounted categories at Best Buy. Premium noise-canceling headphones from Sony and Bose typically drop $100 to $150, bringing flagship models into the $200 to $250 range. True wireless earbuds see similar treatment: expect 30 to 50 percent off AirPods, Galaxy Buds, and Beats. Portable Bluetooth speakers from JBL and Ultimate Ears often hit half price.

Smartwatches are another strong category. Apple Watch models usually see $50 to $100 off, Samsung Galaxy Watches get bundled with free bands or chargers, and Fitbit trackers drop to impulse-buy prices. These are excellent stocking stuffers because the discounts are real and the products rarely disappoint.

Appliances and Smart Home

Best Buy's appliance department quietly runs some of the best Black Friday deals in the store. Robot vacuums from iRobot and Shark drop $150 to $300, stand mixers and espresso machines get 30 to 40 percent off, and large appliance packages (fridge, range, dishwasher bundles) can save you $500 to $1,000 with free delivery and haul-away of old units.

Smart home gear follows: video doorbells, smart thermostats, and security camera kits often drop 30 to 50 percent, and Google Nest and Amazon Echo devices hit their annual low prices. If you are outfitting a new home, the appliance package deals plus smart home bundles can be combined into one very efficient shopping trip.

How to Stack Savings at Best Buy

The sticker price is only the start. Best Buy's loyalty and protection programs can push your real savings higher:

  • My Best Buy members earn points on every purchase and get early access to select deals; the free tier is worth joining before November.
  • Best Buy matches its own prices: if an item drops further during the holiday return window, ask for a price adjustment.
  • Open-box items in excellent condition carry extra discounts on top of sale pricing, and Geek Squad certified open-box gear includes a warranty.
  • The Best Buy credit card often adds 5 percent back in rewards or promotional financing on big purchases.
  • Shop early: Best Buy's early Black Friday deals in the first two weeks of November are often the same prices as the Friday itself, with better stock.
  • Use store pickup to guarantee the item before it sells out online, then grab it on your schedule.

Finally, remember that Best Buy's holiday return window extends into January, so tech gifts are low-risk. Buy the deal when you see it; waiting for a slightly better price usually just means missing the item entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Best Buy Black Friday 2026 deals start?

Best Buy typically launches early Black Friday deals in late October, with the main ad prices going live the week of Thanksgiving and running through Black Friday, November 27, 2026, and into Cyber Monday.

Does Best Buy price match on Black Friday?

Best Buy matches select competitors and its own online prices, but limited-quantity doorbusters and marketplace sellers are usually excluded. Ask at customer service for the current terms.

Are Best Buy's TV deals better online or in store?

Prices are generally identical online and in store. In-store shopping gives you access to open-box deals and lets you take the TV home immediately, while online offers delivery and a wider selection.

What is the My Best Buy program?

My Best Buy is a free loyalty program that earns points on purchases, unlocks member-only deals, and provides early access to select Black Friday offers. Higher tiers add perks like extended returns.

Does Best Buy offer price protection after Black Friday?

Yes, within the return and exchange period, Best Buy will typically adjust the price if an item you purchased drops further. Keep your receipt and ask at the store or via customer service.
