25 Thanksgiving Leftover Recipes 2026: Beyond the Turkey Sandwich

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

The Friday after Thanksgiving is a beautiful problem: a refrigerator packed with turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, gravy, cranberry sauce, and pie. The turkey sandwich is a legend, but after two of them you need reinvention. These 25 leftover recipes turn your Thanksgiving spread into days of genuinely exciting meals, from breakfast to dessert, so nothing goes to waste.

The Golden Rules of Leftover Safety

Before the fun starts, the food safety rules matter. Refrigerate all leftovers within 2 hours of serving dinner. Shredded turkey, stuffing, and mashed potatoes keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge; freeze them if you need longer. Gravy keeps 3 days. Cranberry sauce lasts up to 10 days. Reheat everything to 165 degrees Fahrenheit, and reheat only once. Now, onto the good part.

Turkey Transformations

Shredded turkey is the most versatile leftover in the kitchen. Turkey pot pie is the undisputed champion: fold chopped turkey into a creamy vegetable filling, top with a pie crust or biscuit dough, and bake until golden. Turkey tetrazzini turns turkey, mushrooms, and spaghetti into a creamy baked casserole with a crispy breadcrumb top. For something faster, turkey enchiladas with green sauce and cheese bake in 25 minutes, and turkey fried rice with leftover peas and carrots is a 15-minute weeknight win.

Turkey soup deserves special mention: simmer the turkey carcass for 3 to 4 hours with onions, celery, and carrots for a rich stock, then add shredded meat, egg noodles, and herbs. One carcass makes enough soup for the whole weekend. Turkey chili is another powerhouse; white beans, corn, and green chiles turn a pound of shredded turkey into a bowl that tastes nothing like Thursday.

Stuffing Reinventions

Leftover stuffing is basically a treasure chest. Stuffing waffles are the sleeper hit: press cold stuffing into a greased waffle iron for 4 to 5 minutes until crisp and golden, then top with a fried egg and gravy for the best breakfast of your life. Stuffing-stuffed mushrooms hollow out cremini mushrooms, fill with chopped stuffing, and bake at 375 for 18 minutes. Form leftover stuffing into small patties and pan-fry in butter until crisp for stuffing cakes, which pair perfectly with cranberry sauce as a dip.

Use stuffing as a binder for turkey meatballs, or layer it in a breakfast strata with eggs and cheese, baked at 350 for 40 minutes. It also makes an outstanding base for a savory bread pudding when mixed with beaten eggs, milk, and grated cheese.

Mashed Potatoes, Gravy, and Cranberry Sauce

  • Potato pancakes: Mix cold mashed potatoes with an egg and a little flour, pan-fry in butter until crisp. Top with sour cream or gravy.
  • Shepherd's pie: Layer shredded turkey, corn, and gravy in a baking dish, spread mashed potatoes on top, and bake at 400 until bubbly.
  • Loaded potato soup: Thin mashed potatoes with chicken broth, add cheese, bacon bits, and scallions.
  • Cranberry BBQ sauce: Simmer cranberry sauce with ketchup, mustard, and a splash of vinegar for turkey sliders.
  • Cranberry cream cheese dip: Spread cream cheese on a platter, spoon cranberry sauce over it, and serve with crackers.
  • Turkey cranberry quesadillas: Turkey, brie, and cranberry sauce in a tortilla, grilled until melty.

Breakfast and Brunch Ideas

Thanksgiving leftovers make the best weekend breakfasts. A turkey hash with diced sweet potatoes, onion, and bell pepper, fried in a cast iron skillet and topped with fried eggs, takes 20 minutes and feeds a crowd. Turkey and stuffing breakfast burritos wrap everything with scrambled eggs and cheese for a portable Black Friday morning meal. Sweet potato casserole leftovers become sweet potato pancakes; add an egg and flour, and serve with maple syrup. And leftover pie for breakfast is a tradition that needs no justification.

Freezer Strategy: Thanksgiving in February

If you cooked a big bird, freeze strategically. Portion shredded turkey into 1-pound freezer bags, press flat to remove air, and label with the date. Frozen turkey stays excellent for 3 to 4 months and thaws overnight in the fridge. Freeze gravy in ice cube trays, then transfer the cubes to a bag for single servings. Freeze stuffing and mashed potatoes in portion-sized containers. Come January, you will open the freezer and find a second Thanksgiving waiting. Nothing honors the holiday spirit like refusing to waste a single bite.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Thanksgiving leftovers last in the fridge?

Turkey, stuffing, mashed potatoes, and gravy keep 3 to 4 days refrigerated. Cranberry sauce lasts up to 10 days. Store everything within 2 hours of serving, and reheat leftovers to 165 degrees Fahrenheit.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving leftovers?

Yes. Shredded turkey freezes well for 3 to 4 months in airtight bags. Gravy, stuffing, and mashed potatoes also freeze nicely. Cranberry sauce freezes for up to 2 months. Label everything with the date.

What is the best thing to make with a turkey carcass?

Turkey stock. Simmer the carcass with onion, celery, carrots, and herbs for 3 to 4 hours, strain, and use the stock for soup. It is the single highest-value use of Thanksgiving leftovers.

How do I reheat leftover turkey without drying it out?

Splash the turkey with a little broth or gravy, cover tightly with foil, and warm in a 325 degree oven for 15 to 20 minutes. The moisture and the foil keep it tender.

What can I do with leftover cranberry sauce?

Use it in turkey cranberry quesadillas, as a glaze for meatballs, swirled into oatmeal or yogurt, mixed into a cream cheese dip, or as a base for a tangy BBQ sauce.
