12 Make-Ahead Thanksgiving Casseroles 2026: Easy Holiday Sides

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Casseroles are the backbone of a calm Thanksgiving. They assemble in minutes, feed a crowd from a single dish, and, most importantly, most of them can be fully prepared a day or two before the holiday and finished in the oven while the turkey rests. These 12 make-ahead Thanksgiving casseroles are the classics your guests expect plus a few upgrades, all designed to be prepped early and baked fresh on Thursday.

The Timeless Classics

Green bean casserole remains the most requested Thanksgiving side in America for a reason. Upgrade the canned-soup version by making a quick mushroom cream sauce from scratch; it takes 15 extra minutes and tastes infinitely better. Assemble it fully two days ahead, refrigerate, and add the crispy fried onions just before baking at 375 for 25 minutes. Sweet potato casserole with a brown sugar and pecan streusel topping can be assembled the day before and baked at 350 for 30 minutes. Corn pudding, a custardy blend of corn, eggs, cream, and a little sugar baked at 375 for 45 minutes, is the sleeper hit that disappears first every year.

Comforting Potato and Squash Bakes

  • Scalloped potatoes: Thin-sliced potatoes layered with gruyere and cream, assembled a day ahead, baked at 375 for 60 minutes.
  • Twice-baked potato casserole: All the flavor of twice-baked potatoes in a scoopable 9x13 dish, topped with cheddar and chives.
  • Butternut squash gratin: Roasted squash layered with sage cream and parmesan, finished with breadcrumbs.
  • Mashed potato casserole: Creamy mashed potatoes with cream cheese, baked until golden with a buttered crumb topping.
  • Broccoli cheese casserole: Blanched broccoli in a sharp cheddar sauce with a buttery cracker topping.

Savory Stuffing Bakes and More

A sausage and apple stuffing baked as a casserole in a 9x13 pan, rather than inside the turkey, gives you crispy edges in every serving and can be assembled a full day ahead. A wild mushroom and leek bread pudding with gruyere is the sophisticated cousin of stuffing. For something different, a Brussels sprouts gratin with bacon and fontina converts even the skeptics, and a creamed corn casserole with a cornbread topping bakes into a golden, spoonable side that kids love. Mac and cheese, baked with three cheeses and a panko top at 375 for 30 minutes, is non-negotiable in many families.

Make-Ahead Timing Guide

The make-ahead strategy is simple. Two days before Thanksgiving, assemble casseroles that do not include crispy toppings, cover tightly, and refrigerate. The morning of, pull them out 30 to 45 minutes before baking so they are not ice cold going into the oven; add 10 to 15 minutes to the bake time for a chilled dish. Bake casseroles while the turkey rests, since the oven is free for that 30 to 45 minute window and most casseroles need exactly that. Add any crispy toppings, fried onions, breadcrumbs, or streusel, in the final stretch so they stay crisp. Label each dish with its bake temperature and time on a sticky note; when the kitchen is chaotic, you will thank yourself.

Freezer-Friendly Options

Several of these freeze beautifully if you want to get even further ahead. Sweet potato casserole, corn pudding, and mac and cheese can be assembled, frozen for up to a month, thawed in the fridge for 24 hours, and baked as usual. Green bean casserole freezes well without the onion topping. Scalloped potatoes and stuffing bakes freeze for up to 3 weeks. Write the bake instructions on the foil before freezing, because future-you will not remember. A freezer full of Thanksgiving sides in early November is the ultimate holiday flex.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead can I assemble Thanksgiving casseroles?

Most casseroles can be assembled 1 to 2 days ahead and refrigerated, unbaked. Add crispy toppings like fried onions or breadcrumbs just before baking so they stay crisp.

Should I bake casseroles from cold or room temperature?

Let refrigerated casseroles sit at room temperature 30 to 45 minutes before baking, and add 10 to 15 minutes to the bake time. Baking straight from the fridge can leave the center cold.

When do I bake casseroles on Thanksgiving Day?

Bake them while the turkey rests, which frees the oven for 30 to 45 minutes. Most casseroles need exactly that window at 350 to 375 degrees.

Can I freeze Thanksgiving casseroles?

Yes. Sweet potato casserole, corn pudding, mac and cheese, scalloped potatoes, and stuffing bakes freeze well for 3 to 4 weeks. Thaw in the fridge for 24 hours before baking.

How do I keep casserole toppings crispy when making ahead?

Assemble the casserole base ahead of time but store toppings separately. Add fried onions, breadcrumbs, streusel, or cheese in the last 10 to 15 minutes of baking.
