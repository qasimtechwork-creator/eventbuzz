Christmas Light Tours 2026: Best Holiday Light Displays

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 5 min read

There is nothing quite like driving through a neighborhood transformed into a glowing winter landscape. Christmas light tours, whether organized events or do-it-yourself drives past the best-decorated houses, have become one of December's most beloved low-cost traditions. All you need is a full tank of gas, some hot drinks and a route. Here is how to plan the perfect Christmas light tour for 2026.

How to Find Light Tours Near You

Start with a quick search for your city plus Christmas lights or holiday light display; most metro areas have at least one major drive-through show and several standout neighborhoods. Local news sites and community social media groups usually publish yearly roundups of the best residential displays. Some cities run organized trolley or bus tours with hot cocoa included, which are worth booking early since they sell out. Check dates and ticket prices before you go, since popular drive-through shows often use timed entry.

Plan a DIY Neighborhood Light Tour

The classic version costs nothing: map a route through the best-decorated streets in your area and drive it after dark. Ask around or check local community pages for which streets go all out; every town has them. Pack thermoses of hot chocolate, bring blankets for the back seat, and play Christmas music as you drive. Make it a game for kids by voting on the best house in categories like brightest, funniest and most creative. End the night at a favorite display and take the annual photo.

Famous Holiday Light Displays

A few displays are worth traveling for. Brooklyn's Dyker Heights neighborhood is legendary for its over-the-top residential decorations, all free to walk through. The Rockefeller Center Christmas tree in New York draws crowds every December and pairs well with the city's window displays. Botanical gardens in many cities run evening light shows with illuminated trails, which are magical and stroller-friendly. If you plan a trip around one, go on a weeknight to avoid the worst crowds.

Light Tour Tips

Timing matters: most displays look best between 6 and 9 in the evening, after full dark but before houses start switching off. Weeknights are calmer than weekends at popular spots. Keep headlights on low and drive slowly through residential displays; the homeowners put in serious work and appreciate respectful visitors. Bring cash for donation boxes at charity displays, and never block driveways or park on lawns. If walking a neighborhood display, dress warmly and stay on sidewalks.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Christmas light displays free?

Most residential displays and many city shows are free. Large drive-through events and botanical garden shows usually charge admission, often with timed entry tickets that should be bought in advance.

What is the best time to see Christmas lights?

Between 6 and 9 in the evening is ideal: fully dark, but early enough that most displays are still lit. Many homeowners switch displays off around 10 or 11.

Is it better to walk or drive a light tour?

Drive-through shows require a car, but dense neighborhoods like Dyker Heights are better on foot so you can take your time. For a DIY tour of spread-out streets, driving with the heat on wins.

When do most displays go up?

Most residential and commercial displays are up by early December, with many lighting up the weekend after Thanksgiving. Major shows typically run from late November through early January.
