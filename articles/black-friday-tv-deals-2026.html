Black Friday TV Deals 2026: How to Choose the Right TV

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 7 min read

Few Black Friday categories draw crowds like televisions. TVs are the headline doorbuster almost every year, and 2026 should bring another wave of deep discounts on big screens. But a cheap TV is only a deal if it is the right TV for your room. Here is how to shop smart this Black Friday, on November 27.

When TV Deals Drop

TV prices start falling in October as manufacturers and retailers clear the way for new models, and the discounts accelerate through November. Black Friday week is traditionally the peak — the most aggressive prices of the year on the most popular sizes appear in the days around November 27.

After that, Cyber Monday on November 30 usually extends the best offers online. If you miss both, the next big window is the lead-up to major sporting events early in the new year, when retailers discount TVs again. But for the widest selection at the lowest prices, Black Friday remains the main event.

Screen Size and Room Fit

Bigger is not automatically better. As a rough guide, measure your viewing distance: for 4K TVs, a screen diagonal of about half your viewing distance in inches works well for most living rooms. A 55-inch set suits typical apartments, while larger rooms can comfortably handle 65 inches or more.

Also measure your stand or wall space before you shop — Black Friday excitement has led many buyers to discover their new TV does not fit the entertainment unit. Check the TV's dimensions with the stand attached, not just the screen size.

The Specs That Actually Matter

  • Resolution: 4K is the standard to buy in 2026. Higher resolutions exist but offer diminishing returns for typical viewing distances.
  • HDR support: High Dynamic Range makes a bigger visible difference than resolution alone. Look for broad HDR format support.
  • Refresh rate: Gamers and sports fans should prioritize higher refresh rates for smoother motion; casual viewers will be fine with standard rates.
  • Smart platform: Check that the built-in apps you use are supported, or plan on a streaming stick.
  • Ports: Count your devices — game consoles, soundbars, streaming boxes — and make sure there are enough HDMI inputs.

Display Types in Plain Language

You will see LED, QLED and OLED labels everywhere. In simple terms: standard LED TVs are the most affordable, QLED sets add brighter colors, and OLED panels deliver the deepest blacks and best contrast — usually at a premium. Each has trade-offs in price, brightness and room lighting suitability. Decide based on your room and budget, not on which label sounds most impressive.

How to Spot a Genuine Deal

Watch for holiday-specific model variants: some Black Friday TVs carry slightly different model numbers with trimmed-down features, which makes comparing against reviews tricky. Look up the exact model number before buying. Compare the sale price with what the same or equivalent model sold for in October, check the return policy and warranty terms, and remember that an extended warranty is optional — never let it be bundled in without your consent.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27. TV deals typically ramp up through November, with the deepest prices landing during Black Friday week.

What size TV should I buy?

For 4K TVs, a screen diagonal of roughly half your viewing distance works well for most rooms. Measure your space — including the stand — before you shop.

Are Black Friday TVs lower quality?

Most are standard models at genuine discounts, but some retailers sell holiday-specific variants with slightly different model numbers. Always look up the exact model number and its reviews.

Should I wait for Cyber Monday for TV deals?

Cyber Monday on November 30 usually extends the best Black Friday TV offers online. If the exact model you want is in stock on Black Friday at a good price, though, do not risk waiting.
