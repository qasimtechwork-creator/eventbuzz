When Is Father's Day 2027?

Father's Day 2027 falls on Sunday, June 20, 2027. In the United States and many other countries, the day is observed on the third Sunday of June each year, which keeps it close to the start of summer and easy to gather family around. Mark the date early: cards, restaurant tables and delivery slots for popular gifts all fill quickly in the second week of June.

Many countries share the June date, while others celebrate fathers on different days, including Saint Joseph's Day traditions in parts of Europe and September dates in Australia and New Zealand. If your family lives across borders, a quick check of the local calendar avoids a wish that arrives a season late.

A Short History of Father's Day

The modern Father's Day began with one daughter's determination. Sonora Smart Dodd of Spokane, Washington, was raised with her five siblings by their father, William Jackson Smart, a Civil War veteran who became a single parent. After hearing a Mother's Day sermon in 1909, Dodd believed fathers like hers deserved the same public honour. She campaigned locally for a day of recognition, and Spokane celebrated the first Father's Day on June 19, 1910.

Dodd promoted the idea for decades, and support grew gradually. Presidents including Calvin Coolidge backed observance, and in 1966 President Lyndon B. Johnson issued a presidential proclamation designating the third Sunday in June as Father's Day. In 1972, President Richard Nixon signed it into law as a permanent national day in the United States. What began as one daughter honouring one father's quiet sacrifice is now marked by millions of families every June.

Father's Day Gift Ideas by Type of Dad

The best gift matches the man, not the advertisement. Use these four lenses to find yours, then pair it with a heartfelt line from our Father's Day quotes collection.

The Practical Dad

He values things that work and last. Consider a quality multi-tool, a well-made wallet, a reliable water bottle or thermos, a cordless drill upgrade, or a subscription that removes a small daily annoyance. Practical dads also quietly love services: a car detailed, a watch battery replaced, a garden gate finally fixed.

The Sentimental Dad

He keeps ticket stubs and old drawings. Give him a framed family photograph, a handwritten letter he can reread, a custom print of a place that matters to your family, or a recorded message from grandchildren. Sentimental gifts cost little and are kept forever, which is exactly the point.

The Hobby Dad

He has a world of his own: fishing, woodworking, cycling, cooking, gardening, music or sport. Upgrade one beloved tool, restock his favourite supplies, or book an experience inside his hobby, such as a lesson, a match ticket or a morning on the water together. Joining him in the hobby usually beats any object.

The Budget Dad (and Budget-Conscious Giver)

Some dads insist they want nothing; what they mean is they want no fuss. Honour that with breakfast cooked by you, a card with real sentences in it, a walk together, his favourite film queued up, or a small homemade treat. A thoughtful hour outperforms an expensive hour of shopping, every time.

Simple Ways to Celebrate

Not every Father's Day needs an itinerary. Cook his favourite meal, gather siblings for a call if you live apart, look through old photographs together, or take on one task he always does so he can watch someone else do it for once. If your father has passed away, many families mark the day by visiting a meaningful place, telling his stories aloud, or doing one kind thing in his name. However you observe it, the heart of the day is the same: gratitude, spoken out loud.
