Galentine's Brunch Ideas 2027: Celebrate with Your Girls

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Valentine's Day is for couples, but February 13 belongs to the girls. Galentine's Day, the beloved tradition of celebrating female friendship, deserves a proper brunch: good food, prettier-than-necessary table styling, and zero pressure to be anywhere else. Whether you are hosting two best friends or ten, here is how to make Galentine's brunch 2027 the highlight of the group chat.

The Menu: Sweet, Savory, and Sips

Brunch works best as a mix-and-match spread that guests can graze on for hours. Sweet: heart-shaped waffles or pancakes with strawberry syrup, a yogurt parfait bar with granola and berries, and something chocolate for good measure. Savory: a simple quiche, an avocado toast station with toppings, or breakfast sliders. Sips: mimosas are the classic, but a pink lemonade spritzer, strawberry iced tea, or a fun mocktail keeps it inclusive for everyone. Ask guests to bring one dish each to keep hosting easy, and keep a printed menu card so nobody has to guess what is what.

Table Styling That Pops

Pink, red, and gold is the foolproof palette that photographs beautifully. A tablecloth or runner, fresh flowers in small vases (grocery store roses split into bud vases look expensive), heart-shaped balloons, and a few candles set the mood in minutes. Layer in some confetti or rose petals down the center of the table for texture. Name cards are a lovely touch for bigger groups. Print a simple "Galentine's 2027" sign for the entryway and you have an instant photo backdrop everyone will use.

Games and Activities

A little structure keeps the energy up between courses. Friendship trivia (how well do you really know each other?) always gets loud, a "roast and toast" round where everyone shares one compliment and one funny memory is heartwarming, and a DIY flower crown or friendship bracelet station gives hands something to do. A group photo with props is mandatory, so set out silly glasses and heart wands. Keep activities optional and short so conversation stays the main event.

Hosting Tips: Guest List and Timing

Invite two weeks out and cap the guest list at what your table can actually seat, because squeezing in extra chairs kills the vibe. An 11 AM start gives everyone time to arrive looking human and lets the brunch stretch into the afternoon. Prep the night before: set the table, mix dry ingredients, arrange flowers, and chill the drinks. Morning-of, just cook and pour. Have a simple playlist ready before the first guest arrives. And remember the golden rule of Galentine's: no plus-ones, no phones at the table, and everybody leaves feeling celebrated.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Galentine's Day 2027?

Galentine's Day is February 13 every year, the day before Valentine's Day. In 2027 it falls on a Saturday.

What do you serve at a Galentine's brunch?

A mix of sweet and savory: waffles or pancakes, quiche or breakfast sliders, fruit, and festive drinks like mimosas or pink spritzers.

How many people should I invite to Galentine's brunch?

Whatever your table can comfortably seat, usually 4 to 10. Intimate groups have better conversation than big crowds.

What is a good Galentine's brunch theme?

Pink and red with gold accents is classic. Other fun options: pajama brunch, Parisian cafe, or a flower-crown garden party.
