Christmas Morning Breakfast Ideas 2026: Easy Recipes to Start the Day

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 4 min read

Christmas morning runs on excitement, wrapping paper, and — if you plan ahead — a breakfast that practically serves itself. The secret to a joyful December 25th morning is doing the work the night before, so you can watch the present-opening instead of standing over the stove. These ideas keep everyone fed and happy with minimal morning effort.

Make-Ahead Breakfast Casseroles

The ultimate Christmas morning strategy: assemble tonight, bake tomorrow.

  • Overnight French toast bake: Cube a loaf of brioche or challah, soak in an egg-milk mixture with cinnamon and vanilla overnight, then bake until golden. Serve with maple syrup and berries.
  • Egg and sausage casserole: Layer bread cubes, cooked sausage, cheese, and beaten eggs in a dish; refrigerate overnight and bake in the morning while gifts are opened.
  • Hash brown casserole: Shredded potatoes, eggs, cheese, and diced peppers — hearty enough to fuel a full morning of festivities.

All three go from fridge to oven in one step, freeing you for the important work of handing out presents.

Pancake and Waffle Bar

Set out a stack of plain pancakes or waffles (or a batter ready to cook) with a topping bar: maple syrup, whipped cream, fresh berries, chocolate chips, sliced bananas, and chopped nuts. Kids love building their own, and it accommodates every preference at the table. For a festive touch, add red and green sprinkles or shape pancakes with holiday cookie cutters.

Sweet Showstoppers

  • Cinnamon rolls: Use refrigerated dough for a 20-minute version, or prep homemade rolls the night before for a second rise in the morning. Warm cream cheese frosting is non-negotiable.
  • Stollen or panettone: These traditional European Christmas breads need no prep at all — slice and serve with butter and coffee.
  • Monkey bread: Pull-apart dough balls baked with cinnamon sugar; fun to eat and impressive on the table.

Festive Morning Drinks

Drinks make the morning feel special with almost no effort:

  • Hot chocolate bar: A slow cooker of hot cocoa with marshmallows, candy canes, whipped cream, and cocoa powder for dusting.
  • Christmas punch: Cranberry juice, ginger ale, and orange slices over ice — festive, kid-friendly, and easy.
  • Fancy coffee: Add peppermint or vanilla syrup to the regular coffee pot for a holiday upgrade adults will appreciate.

A Simple Christmas Morning Timeline

  • Christmas Eve night: Assemble any overnight casserole, set the table, lay out the topping bar ingredients, and program the coffee maker.
  • First wake-up: Preheat the oven and put the casserole in before the first gift is opened.
  • Midway through gifts: Start pancakes or pull cinnamon rolls from the oven; set out the drink station.
  • After gifts: Sit down together for a real breakfast — the wrapping paper can wait.

The goal isn't a perfect brunch; it's a relaxed morning where nobody is hungry, stressed, or stuck in the kitchen. Plan ahead, keep it simple, and enjoy the best morning of the year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good make-ahead Christmas breakfast?

Overnight French toast bake and egg-and-sausage breakfast casseroles are the classics: assemble them on Christmas Eve, refrigerate overnight, and bake in the morning. They feed a crowd with almost no morning work.

How do I keep Christmas morning breakfast stress-free?

Do everything possible the night before — assemble casseroles, set the table, lay out toppings, program the coffee maker. Choose one hot dish plus simple sides rather than cooking multiple things to order.

What do you serve for Christmas brunch drinks?

A hot chocolate bar in a slow cooker, cranberry-ginger Christmas punch, and coffee with peppermint or vanilla syrup cover all ages. Mimosas or a sparkling cider toast work for the adults.

Can I prepare pancakes ahead of time?

Yes — cook pancakes the night before, cool them, and stack with parchment paper between each. Reheat in a warm oven for 10 minutes in the morning. Or keep it simple with a topping bar and cook fresh batter while gifts are being opened.
