20 Christmas Cookie Recipes for the 2026 Holiday Season

By EventBuzz Team • October 19, 2026 • 7 min read

The smell of Christmas cookies baking is pure holiday magic. Whether you are building a cookie exchange platter, leaving treats for Santa, or just filling the house with cinnamon and vanilla, this collection of 20 Christmas cookie recipes for 2026 has something for every baker — from foolproof classics to impressive showstoppers.

Classic Cut-Out Cookies

Sugar cookies with royal icing are the decorating canvas of Christmas. Gingerbread — soft or snappy — plus snickerdoodles rolled in cinnamon sugar round out the must-bake trio. Chill dough well for sharp shapes and clean edges.

Chocolate Lovers' Favorites

Double chocolate crinkles crackle beautifully with powdered sugar. Peppermint bark cookies layer chocolate and crushed candy cane. Hot cocoa cookies with marshmallow centers taste like the drink in cookie form.

Baker's secret: weigh your flour with a kitchen scale instead of scooping. Scooped flour can vary by 30 percent, which is the hidden cause of most dry, crumbly holiday cookies.

Old-World European Treats

Linzer cookies with jam centers, Italian pizzelle, German lebkuchen and Russian tea cakes bring heritage flavor to your platter. These keep beautifully, making them ideal for gifting and shipping.

Showstoppers and New Twists

Stained-glass cookies with candy centers glow in window light. Eggnog snickerdoodles, cranberry-orange shortbread and salted caramel thumbprints give classics a 2026 twist. Finish with a dusting of edible glitter for bakery-case shine.

Do not forget presentation: stack cookies in clear cellophane bags tied with ribbon, or arrange them on a vintage platter lined with parchment. Beautiful packaging turns a simple batch into a gift people rave about.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead can I bake Christmas cookies?

Most cookies keep 1 to 2 weeks in airtight containers. Freeze dough balls or baked cookies up to 3 months for stress-free December baking.

Why do my sugar cookies spread?

Warm dough and too-soft butter are the usual culprits. Chill cut shapes 15 minutes before baking and check your oven temperature.

What is the best cookie for exchanges?

Sturdy, distinctive cookies win: gingerbread, shortbread, biscotti and decorated sugar cookies travel well and look impressive.

How do I keep cookies soft?

Store with a slice of bread in the container — the cookies absorb its moisture. Keep crisp cookies separate from soft ones.

Conclusion

Pick three or four recipes, bake in batches, and share generously — that is the whole secret of Christmas cookies. Flour on your sweater, carols on the speaker, and a house that smells like December. Happy baking!
