Black Friday Appliance Deals 2026: Save Big on Home Essentials

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Black Friday on November 27, 2026 is the single best day of the year to buy appliances. Retailers discount major appliances by hundreds of dollars, bundle kitchen and laundry packages for four-figure savings, and offer free delivery and haul-away that normally cost extra. Whether you are replacing a dying refrigerator or outfitting a whole kitchen, the strategy is the same: shop early in November, compare package pricing, and lock in delivery before the holiday rush.

Refrigerators, Ranges and Dishwashers

Major kitchen appliances see the deepest Black Friday cuts of any home category. French-door refrigerators typically drop $400 to $800 off, with last year's models discounted even further as stores clear floor space. Electric and gas ranges fall $200 to $500, and dishwashers from Bosch, Samsung, and LG land in the $350 to $550 range, down from $600 to $900.

The real savings come from package deals: buying a fridge, range, dishwasher, and microwave together from one retailer often triggers an extra 10 to 15 percent off the already-discounted prices, plus free delivery and installation. That can total $1,000 to $2,000 in savings on a full kitchen set. Measure your spaces carefully before buying, because holiday delivery slots fill fast and returns on large appliances are complicated.

Washers and Dryers

Laundry pairs are a Black Friday classic. Front-load washer and dryer sets that retail for $1,600 to $2,000 typically drop to $1,000 to $1,300, and top-load pairs fall into the $800 to $1,100 range. Retailers sweeten the deal with free pedestal installation, extended warranties at half price, and haul-away of your old machines.

If only one machine needs replacing, buying the pair is still often cheaper than a single unit at regular price, thanks to the pair discounts. Check the energy ratings too: a high-efficiency model bought on sale can pay for part of itself in lower utility bills. Stackable and compact units for apartments see smaller but still meaningful discounts of $100 to $200.

Small Kitchen Appliances

Small appliances are the impulse buys of Black Friday, and the discounts are real. Stand mixers from KitchenAid typically drop $100 to $150, landing around $250 to $350 for the classic models. Air fryers, espresso machines, and multi-cookers fall 30 to 45 percent, with brand-name models hitting $60 to $150.

Coffee gear deserves special attention: drip machines, espresso makers, and grinders from Breville, De'Longhi, and Nespresso routinely hit their lowest prices of the year. These make excellent gifts, and retailers bundle them with free coffee pods, filters, or accessory kits during Black Friday week. Blenders and food processors from Vitamix, Ninja, and Cuisinart round out the category with cuts of $50 to $200.

Vacuums and Floor Care

Robot vacuums are one of the most discounted products of the entire Black Friday season. Models from iRobot, Shark, Roborock, and Eufy drop $150 to $350, with solid midrange robots landing under $250. Stick vacuums from Dyson and Shark see cuts of $100 to $200, and the flagship cordless models that rarely go on sale get genuine Black Friday pricing.

Carpet cleaners, steam mops, and wet-dry vacuums follow with 25 to 40 percent discounts. If you have pets, look for the self-emptying robot models, which see the steepest cuts because retailers want to move premium inventory. Replacement filters and bags bundled free are a common sweetener, so check what is included before comparing prices.

Tips for Buying Appliances on Black Friday

Appliances are big purchases with big delivery logistics. Shop smart:

  • Start in early November: many retailers run the same appliance prices for the whole month, with better delivery availability early on.
  • Ask about package pricing: buying two or more major appliances together usually unlocks an extra percentage off.
  • Confirm delivery details: free delivery, installation, and haul-away are common Black Friday perks, but they are not automatic at every store.
  • Measure twice: doorways, stairwells, and the installation space. A great deal is useless if the fridge does not fit.
  • Check the warranty: extended protection plans are often half price during Black Friday week.
  • Compare the final price: factor in delivery, installation, haul-away, and tax, not just the sticker price.

Finally, ask about price protection. Many appliance retailers will refund the difference if the price drops further within the holiday return window, so buying early in November costs you nothing and secures your delivery slot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Black Friday really the cheapest time to buy appliances?

Yes. Major appliances see their deepest discounts of the year during Black Friday week, with package deals adding another 10 to 15 percent off. Holiday weekends in September and January sales are the only close competitors.

Do appliance package deals apply on Black Friday?

Yes, and they stack with sale prices. Buying a refrigerator, range, dishwasher, and microwave together typically unlocks extra savings plus free delivery and installation.

How early should I buy appliances for Black Friday?

Many retailers run Black Friday appliance pricing for the entire month of November. Buying in early November secures better delivery slots at the same prices.

Is delivery and installation free during Black Friday?

Free delivery is common during Black Friday week at major retailers, and many include basic installation and haul-away of old appliances. Confirm the details at checkout, as policies vary.

Can I return an appliance bought on Black Friday?

Large appliances have stricter return policies than small items, often 15 to 30 days, and may carry restocking fees. Verify measurements and the return terms before purchasing.
