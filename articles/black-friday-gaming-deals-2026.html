Black Friday Gaming Deals 2026: Consoles, Games and Accessories

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

For gamers, Black Friday on November 27, 2026 is the one day of the year when consoles, games, and accessories all hit their lowest prices at once. Retailers compete fiercely for gaming dollars, which means deep discounts on hardware bundles, half-price blockbuster titles, and major cuts on headsets, controllers, and storage. Whether you are buying your first console or upgrading a battlestation, this guide breaks down where the real deals are.

Console Deals and Bundles

Consoles rarely get direct price cuts outside the holidays, which makes Black Friday the moment to buy. Expect bundle deals that pair a console with an extra controller, a game, or a subscription card at no extra cost, effectively saving you $60 to $100. Retailers like Best Buy, Target, Walmart, and GameStop typically run their console offers as doorbusters with limited stock, so decide which platform you want ahead of time.

The Nintendo Switch family is a Black Friday staple: the standard Switch and Switch Lite usually get bundle treatment with a free game or accessory pack. PlayStation and Xbox consoles see retailer-exclusive bundles rather than straight discounts, so compare what is included, not just the price. If you are patient, open-box consoles at electronics retailers can add another 10 to 15 percent off the sale price.

Video Game Discounts

Black Friday is when new releases finally drop in price. Expect this year's biggest titles to fall to $30 to $40, last year's hits to hit $20 or less, and evergreen favorites to get bundle pricing. Digital storefronts run parallel sales, so check the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, and Nintendo eShop alongside physical retailers; digital prices sometimes undercut the discs.

Physical games see buy-two-get-one-free promotions at Target and Best Buy almost every Black Friday, which is the best per-game price you will find. Collectors should watch GameStop for trade-in bonuses that stack with sale prices. And if you are gifting, remember that many retailers extend holiday returns into January, so a game bought in November can be exchanged if it is a duplicate.

Gaming Headsets, Controllers and Accessories

Accessories deliver some of the steepest percentage discounts in gaming. Premium wireless headsets from brands like SteelSeries, HyperX, and Turtle Beach typically drop 30 to 50 percent, bringing $150 headsets into the $75 to $100 range. Extra controllers, charging docks, and elite pro controllers see cuts of 20 to 40 percent across all platforms.

Gaming keyboards and mice are heavily discounted too, with mechanical keyboards and high-DPI mice often half price. Storage is another win: external SSDs and expansion cards for consoles drop 25 to 40 percent, which matters when modern games eat 100GB each. Chairs, desks, and monitor arms round out the setup deals, with gaming furniture marked down 20 to 35 percent.

PC Gaming Deals

PC gamers get their own Black Friday bonanza. Gaming laptops with current-generation GPUs typically drop $200 to $400, prebuilt desktops see similar cuts, and individual components like graphics cards, SSDs, and RAM hit annual lows as retailers clear inventory. Monitors are a highlight: high-refresh-rate 1440p gaming monitors often fall into the $180 to $250 range.

If you are building, price out the full system across two or three retailers, because no single store wins on every component. CPU and motherboard combo deals at electronics retailers can save an extra $50 to $100 over buying parts separately. Peripherals bought together, keyboard, mouse, and headset from one brand, often unlock bundle pricing.

Where to Shop for the Best Gaming Prices

Gaming deals are spread across more stores than any other category, so a little comparison shopping pays off:

  • Best Buy: strongest on consoles, gaming laptops, and headset doorbusters, plus open-box discounts.
  • Target: buy-two-get-one-free game deals and strong Switch bundle pricing.
  • Walmart: aggressive console bundle pricing and online-only flash deals.
  • GameStop: trade-in bonuses and exclusive collector bundles, plus membership perks.
  • Amazon: matches most prices and wins on accessories, storage, and PC components.
  • Digital storefronts: PlayStation Store, Xbox, Steam, and eShop sales for download-only shoppers.

Set up deal alerts in early November, because the best gaming doorbusters routinely sell out within hours. If a bundle you want disappears, check back on Cyber Monday, when many of the same prices return for the online-only event.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do consoles actually get cheaper on Black Friday?

Direct price cuts are rare, but retailers add value through bundles: extra controllers, games, or subscription cards included at the standard price, saving you $60 to $100 versus buying separately.

When do Black Friday gaming deals start in 2026?

Early gaming deals appear in the first two weeks of November, with the deepest discounts and doorbuster bundles going live Thanksgiving week and running through Black Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday.

Are digital game sales better than physical deals?

It varies. Physical retailers run buy-two-get-one-free offers that beat digital per-game prices, but digital storefront sales win for convenience and on older titles. Compare both before buying.

Which retailer has the best gaming headset deals?

Best Buy and Amazon consistently offer the deepest headset discounts, typically 30 to 50 percent off premium models. Target and Walmart match on popular midrange options.
