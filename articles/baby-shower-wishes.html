Baby Shower Wishes & Messages for the Mom to Be

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

A baby shower is a room full of people who already love a baby they have not met yet. Your card is the part of the day parents keep and reread, during the happy countdown and later on tired nights. The best wishes are warm, short and personal: celebrate the mom to be, welcome the little one, and offer your love. Every message below was written fresh, sorted for a boy, a girl, friends and family, so you can find the right words in a minute and make them yours with one personal detail.

Quick answer: A perfect baby shower wish congratulates the mom to be, welcomes the baby, and adds one loving promise. Short on time? Write: "Congratulations! Your baby is already so loved, and so are you. We cannot wait to meet your little one."

Wishes for the Mom to Be

These lines put her at the centre, because the shower is her celebration of courage, patience and love.

  • Congratulations, beautiful mom to be. The love you already carry for this baby is the sweetest thing in this room.
  • You are about to meet someone who will think you hung the moon. Enjoy every second of this special wait.
  • Wishing you a calm, healthy and joyful last stretch of pregnancy, and a baby who sleeps almost as well as you deserve.
  • May your delivery be safe, your recovery gentle, and your arms full of the sweetest little blessing very soon.
  • To the mom to be: you were made for this, and you will never walk this road alone. We are all right here.
  • Soon you will hold in your arms the person you have been loving sight unseen. What a miracle that is. Congratulations.

Wishes for a Baby Boy

Blue balloons in the room? These messages welcome the little gentleman on his way.

  • A little boy is on his way to steal hearts and midnight sleep. Congratulations to the whole family!
  • Welcome, baby boy. You are arriving into a home full of laughter, and parents who already adore you.
  • May this little boy grow up kind, brave and curious, with his mother’s heart and his family’s sense of fun.
  • Here comes the son who will turn ordinary days into stories worth telling for decades. So happy for you all.

Wishes for a Baby Girl

Soft and joyful lines for the little girl everyone is waiting to meet.

  • A baby girl is on her way, and the world just got a little brighter. Congratulations, dear friends.
  • Welcome, little princess. You are already loved by more people than you will ever be able to count.
  • May this baby girl grow up strong, kind and full of wonder, just like the wonderful mother she is coming to.
  • Here she comes to fill your home with giggles and your hearts with a whole new kind of love.

Wishes from Friends

Friend wishes can be lighter, warmer and a little playful, with a real offer of help tucked inside.

  • From late night talks to late night feeds, I am in for all of it. Congratulations, my favourite mom to be.
  • Your baby is gaining the fun auntie or uncle package absolutely free. Cannot wait to spoil that little one with you.
  • We celebrated every milestone together so far, and this is the best one yet. So proud of you, congratulations.
  • Consider this my official promise: coffee deliveries, baby cuddles and listening ears, on call from day one.
  • Our group chat is about to become ninety percent baby photos, and I have never been happier about anything.

Wishes from Family

Family messages carry generations of love, blessing and a promise to stand close in every season.

  • Our family tree is growing its sweetest branch yet. Congratulations, we are counting the days with you.
  • A new baby in this family means new laughter at every gathering for the rest of our lives. We are so grateful.
  • We prayed for this moment, and now we get to watch it arrive. May mother and baby be blessed with perfect health.
  • The same hands that once held you will soon hold your baby. Some joys are too big for words. We love you.

What to Write in a Baby Shower Card

A lovely card has four beats. Begin with congratulations to the mom to be by name. Add one line welcoming the baby, or a sweet mystery if the gender is a surprise. Include one personal touch: a memory, a quality you admire, or an offer of help like meals or errands. Close with your name and love. Two to four sentences is plenty; sincere and specific beats long and flowery. Parents keep these cards for years, so write words you would be happy for the baby to read one day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best short baby shower wish?

"Congratulations! Your baby is already so loved, and so are you. We cannot wait to meet your little one." It is warm, complete and suits almost any relationship.

Should I write to the mom or to the baby?

Either works. Most guests address the mom to be first, since the shower celebrates her, then add a welcome line for the baby. Writing a few words directly to the baby is a sweet touch parents treasure.

What if I do not know the baby’s gender?

Use joyful neutral words like little one, baby dear or sweet blessing. Every wish in the mom to be, friends and family sections above works perfectly without mentioning a boy or a girl.

Is it okay to add a small joke in the card?

A gentle joke between close friends is fine, but keep the card itself kind and timeless. Save the teasing for conversation, since the card may be kept in a memory box for decades.

Can I copy these wishes for my card or message?

Yes. Every wish is original to EventBuzz and free for personal use in cards and messages. Add the parents’ names to make it personal.

Conclusion

The wish that matters sounds like you and arrives with love. Pick a line, add a name and one honest promise, and it will be reread on joyful days and long nights alike.
