Christmas Wishes for Coworkers, Boss & Clients 2026

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Workplace Christmas wishes walk a friendly line: warm enough to feel human, polished enough for the whole office to read. This guide gives you formal greetings for clients, friendly lines for your team, respectful notes for your boss, and email sign-offs you can paste in seconds. Christmas Day 2026 is Friday, December 25 — send workplace wishes before offices close for the break. For the card-writing formula itself, see what to write in a Christmas card.

Quick answer: The safest professional wish is warm and short: "Merry Christmas and thank you for a great year of teamwork. Wishing you and your family a restful holiday and a strong start to 2027." For mixed or international teams, "Season's greetings and warm wishes for the new year" keeps everyone included.

Formal Christmas Wishes for Clients & Business Contacts

Grateful, polished, and free of anything personal. These suit cards, emails and year-end thank-you notes.

  • Season's greetings, and thank you for your trust and partnership this year. Wishing you a joyful Christmas and a successful 2027.
  • Merry Christmas. It has been a pleasure working with you in 2026, and we look forward to everything the new year brings.
  • Warm wishes for Christmas and the year ahead. Thank you for choosing to work with us.
  • With appreciation for a great year of collaboration, we wish you and your family a peaceful Christmas.
  • Happy holidays from our team to yours. May your Christmas be restful and your new year prosperous.
  • Thank you for a wonderful year. Wishing you joy this Christmas and continued success in 2027.

Friendly Christmas Wishes for Coworkers & Your Team

A little more sparkle is safe here — these are the people who survived the deadlines with you.

  • Merry Christmas, team! Thanks for the laughs, the saves and the snacks. Enjoy every minute of the break.
  • Wishing you a Christmas with zero meetings, minimal emails and maximum dessert. We earned it.
  • Merry Christmas! Grateful for a team that makes even the hard weeks feel doable.
  • May your Christmas be restful, your inbox be silent, and your return be gently scheduled. Happy holidays!
  • To my favourite coworkers: thanks for another year of teamwork and truly excellent coffee runs. Merry Christmas!
  • Hope your holidays are as smooth as our best project this year. Merry Christmas!

Christmas Wishes for Your Boss or Manager

Respectful and appreciative, without overdoing it. One specific thanks makes it memorable.

  • Merry Christmas, and thank you for your guidance this year. Wishing you and your family a restful, joyful holiday.
  • Thank you for leading the team through a big year with good humour. Merry Christmas — enjoy a well-earned break.
  • Wishing you a peaceful Christmas. I appreciate everything I have learned working with you in 2026.
  • Merry Christmas! Thank you for the opportunities and the trust this year. Looking forward to 2027.

Short Email Sign-Off Lines

Paste one above your signature in the last working days of December.

  • Wishing you a Merry Christmas and a restful break.
  • Warm wishes for the holidays and the new year.
  • Season's greetings, and thank you for a great year.
  • Merry Christmas — see you in 2027.
  • With thanks and festive wishes.
  • Enjoy the holidays; you have earned them.

Lines for the Office Group Card

Short, cheerful and safe when the card is doing the rounds.

  • Merry Christmas, everyone — thanks for a brilliant year!
  • Wishing the whole team joy, rest and excellent leftovers.
  • Grateful to work with you all. Happy holidays!
  • Here is to a calm Christmas and a cracking 2027.

What to Avoid in Workplace Wishes

Skip jokes about workload, salaries, specific religions or anyone's personal life. Avoid sending religious wording to large mixed groups unless you know it is welcome — "Season's greetings" and "Happy holidays" include everyone gracefully. And send before the office closes: a wish that arrives on the first day back feels like homework, not cheer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I wish a client Merry Christmas professionally?

Keep it grateful and forward-looking: "Season's greetings, and thank you for your trust and partnership this year. Wishing you a joyful Christmas and a successful 2027." One or two sentences is plenty.

What should I write to my boss at Christmas?

Thank them for something specific — guidance, an opportunity, support on a project — then wish them a restful holiday with their family. Appreciation is always appropriate; effusiveness is not required.

Is "Merry Christmas" okay at work, or should I say "Happy holidays"?

Both are fine. "Merry Christmas" suits people you know celebrate it; "Happy holidays" or "Season's greetings" is the graceful choice for large, mixed or international teams. When in doubt, warm and inclusive wins.

When should I send Christmas wishes at work?

During the final working week before the break, and ideally before the office closes. For clients in other time zones or countries, send a few days earlier so your message lands inside their working days.

What is a good subject line for a Christmas email?

"Season's Greetings from [Your Name/Team]" is clean and professional. "Thank You & Merry Christmas" works beautifully for clients you have worked with closely all year.

Conclusion

A workplace wish is a small investment with a long return: two warm sentences in December are remembered all year. Choose your section, keep it sincere, and send before the lights go out on the office. Merry Christmas 2026 from EventBuzz.
