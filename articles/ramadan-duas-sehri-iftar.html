Ramadan Duas: Essential Duas for Sehri, Iftar & Forgiveness

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Ramadan runs on dua. The quiet words at sehri, the grateful words at iftar and the hopeful words of the last nights are what turn hunger into worship. This guide gathers the well known daily duas of the month with Arabic text, simple transliteration and an English translation, so you can read, understand and mean every word. For the expected month calendar, see our Ramadan 2027 dates guide.

Quick answer: The core daily duas are the sehri intention, the iftar dua beginning "Allahumma laka sumtu", the three ashra duas for mercy, forgiveness and protection, and the Laylatul Qadr dua "Allahumma innaka afuwwun". All are written out in full below.

Dua of Intention at Sehri

Before the fast begins, many Muslims state their intention with this well known dua. The intention in the heart is what matters most, and these words simply give it a voice.

وَبِصَوْمِ غَدٍ نَوَيْتُ مِنْ شَهْرِ رَمَضَانَ

Wa bisawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri Ramadan.

Translation: I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan.

Iftar Dua: Breaking the Fast

At sunset, after a long day of patience, the fast is broken with gratitude. This is the most widely recited iftar dua around the world.

اللَّهُمَّ لَكَ صُمْتُ وَبِكَ آمَنْتُ وَعَلَيْكَ تَوَكَّلْتُ وَعَلَى رِزْقِكَ أَفْطَرْتُ

Allahumma laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa alayka tawakkaltu wa ala rizqika aftartu.

Translation: O Allah, I fasted for You, I believe in You, I put my trust in You, and with Your provision I break my fast.

Another authentic report teaches the words said after the first sip of water: the thirst has gone, the veins are moistened, and the reward is confirmed, if Allah wills. The moments just before iftar are considered a precious time when duas are answered, so ask freely in your own words too.

Duas for the Three Ashras

Ramadan is often described in three stages of ten days, called ashras: mercy, forgiveness and protection from the Fire. Many families recite a short dua for each stage, alongside their own personal prayers.

First Ashra: Days of Mercy

رَبِّي اغْفِرْ وَارْحَمْ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ الرَّاحِمِينَ

Rabbi ighfir warham wa anta khayrur rahimeen.

Translation: My Lord, forgive and have mercy, and You are the best of those who show mercy.

Second Ashra: Days of Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ رَبِّي مِنْ كُلِّ ذَنْبٍ وَأَتُوبُ إِلَيْهِ

Astaghfirullaha rabbi min kulli dhanbin wa atubu ilayh.

Translation: I seek forgiveness from Allah, my Lord, for every sin, and I turn to Him in repentance.

Third Ashra: Days of Protection

اللَّهُمَّ أَجِرْنِي مِنَ النَّارِ

Allahumma ajirni minan nar.

Translation: O Allah, protect me from the Fire.

The Laylatul Qadr Dua

When Aisha, may Allah be pleased with her, asked the Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him, what to say on the Night of Power, he taught her this short and beautiful dua. It is the single most important dua of the last ten nights, explained fully in our Laylatul Qadr guide.

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّكَ عَفُوٌّ تُحِبُّ الْعَفْوَ فَاعْفُ عَنِّي

Allahumma innaka afuwwun tuhibbul afwa fa'fu anni.

Translation: O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love pardon, so pardon me.

Frequently Asked Questions

What dua is read before starting the fast?

Many people recite "Wa bisawmi ghadin nawaytu min shahri Ramadan", meaning I intend to keep the fast for tomorrow in the month of Ramadan. The intention in the heart is the essential part.

What is the main iftar dua?

"Allahumma laka sumtu wa bika aamantu wa alayka tawakkaltu wa ala rizqika aftartu": O Allah, I fasted for You, I believe in You, I trust in You, and with Your provision I break my fast.

What are the three ashra duas?

The first ashra dua asks for mercy, the second seeks forgiveness with "Astaghfirullaha rabbi min kulli dhanbin wa atubu ilayh", and the third asks for protection with "Allahumma ajirni minan nar".

What dua should I repeat on Laylatul Qadr?

"Allahumma innaka afuwwun tuhibbul afwa fa'fu anni": O Allah, You are Pardoning and You love pardon, so pardon me. Repeat it often through the odd nights of the last ten days.

Can I make dua in my own language?

Yes. The Arabic duas carry the words taught and loved for centuries, and personal dua in your own language, from your own heart, is equally encouraged at sehri, before iftar and in the night prayers.

Conclusion

Learn one dua at a time, understand its meaning, and let it slow you down for a moment each day. A Ramadan spoken with attention is a Ramadan remembered for life.
