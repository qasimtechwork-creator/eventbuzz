Target Black Friday Deals 2026: Best Buys for Holiday Shopping

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Black Friday falls on November 27, 2026, and Target is once again one of the best places to shop it. The retailer blends competitive prices on electronics and toys with deals on home goods, apparel, and holiday decor that rival specialty stores. Unlike some big-box competitors, Target runs its Black Friday pricing across both its website and its nearly 2,000 stores, and most online deals are available for same-day pickup or free shipping. This guide covers when the deals start, the categories worth watching, and the tactics that get you the lowest prices.

When Do Target's Black Friday 2026 Deals Start?

Target rarely waits for the Friday itself. In recent years the retailer has rolled out early Black Friday deals starting in late October, with new waves dropping each week through November. Expect the first big wave of electronics and toy markdowns around the first week of November, a second wave of deeper discounts mid-month, and the official Black Friday ad prices going live the Sunday before Thanksgiving. Most of the headline deals run online and in store at the same time, so you are not forced to camp out at dawn.

Target stores are typically closed on Thanksgiving Day and reopen early Friday morning, often at 7 a.m. local time. If you prefer to avoid the crowd entirely, shop Target.com on Thanksgiving evening, when the online deals usually match the in-store doorbuster prices. Quantities on the hottest items are limited, so the deepest discounts on TVs, gaming consoles, and Apple products can sell out within hours of going live.

Best Tech Deals to Watch at Target

Target's electronics deals consistently rank among the strongest of any retailer, especially on televisions and Apple gear. Look for 55-inch 4K TVs dropping into the $230 to $280 range, 65-inch models under $400, and bundle offers that add a free gift card to big-ticket purchases. Apple products rarely see direct discounts at the Apple Store, but Target typically cuts $50 to $150 off AirPods, $30 to $75 off Apple Watches, and offers gift-card bundles on iPads.

Laptops and tablets follow a similar pattern: Chromebooks for under $150, midrange Windows laptops marked down 25 to 40 percent, and Amazon devices such as Echo speakers and Fire tablets at their lowest prices of the year. Headphones and earbuds from Sony, Bose, and Beats usually see cuts of 30 to 50 percent. If you are shopping for wearables, compare Target's price against its own gift-card promotions, which often beat straight discounts once you factor in the card.

Toys and Kids' Deals

Target is a toy destination at Black Friday, and its deals often beat Amazon on the biggest brands. Watch for 30 to 50 percent off LEGO sets, Barbie, Hot Wheels, and Fisher-Price, plus buy-two-get-one-free promotions on board games and video games. Store-exclusive toy bundles and advent calendars also tend to sell through quickly, so add them to your cart early and check out fast.

For bigger gifts, Target usually discounts ride-on toys, play kitchens, and dollhouses by $30 to $80 off. Kids' apparel and shoes from Cat and Jack and All in Motion get holiday markdowns too, which is ideal if you are stocking up on winter basics alongside the fun stuff. Keep an eye on the weekly ad for doorbuster toy deals that appear only in the Sunday circular.

Home, Kitchen and Holiday Deals

Black Friday at Target is quietly excellent for home goods. Small kitchen appliances like stand mixers, air fryers, and coffee makers typically drop 30 to 45 percent, and Target's own brands like Threshold and Hearth and Hand get deep holiday discounts on bedding, bath, and decor. Artificial Christmas trees, string lights, and outdoor holiday decorations often hit 40 to 50 percent off in the weeks before Thanksgiving.

Furniture markdowns are worth a look if you are refreshing a room: accent chairs, bookshelves, and TV stands frequently see 20 to 35 percent discounts. Vacuum cleaners from Dyson and Shark are Black Friday staples at Target, with cuts of $100 or more. These larger items are all eligible for free shipping or order pickup, which saves you a delivery fee.

How to Score Target's Best Doorbusters

Target rewards shoppers who plan ahead. Use these tactics to get the lowest prices and avoid missing out:

  • Join Target Circle (free) and opt into the holiday deal alerts so you get notified the moment new deals drop.
  • Check Target's price adjustment policy: if an item you bought drops in price during the holiday window, you can usually get the difference refunded.
  • Stack deals with the RedCard debit option, which takes an extra 5 percent off most purchases.
  • Shop the gift card deals: Target often sells discounted gift cards during Black Friday week, effectively cutting your costs further.
  • Use order pickup or same-day delivery to lock in online prices without worrying about shipping cutoffs.
  • Read the Sunday ad for doorbusters that never appear in the online preview; some are in-store only.

One more tip: Target's holiday return window is extended through early January, so you can buy gifts with confidence. Keep your receipts in the Target app, which stores them digitally and makes returns at any store painless.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Target's Black Friday 2026 sale start?

Early Black Friday deals usually begin in late October, with new waves each week in November. The official Black Friday ad prices go live the Sunday before Thanksgiving, November 22, 2026, and run through Friday, November 27.

Does Target price match during Black Friday?

Target matches select competitors' prices on identical items, but Black Friday doorbuster pricing and limited-quantity offers are typically excluded. Check the current policy at guest services before assuming a match.

What time do Target stores open on Black Friday 2026?

Most Target stores open at 7 a.m. local time on Black Friday. Online deals usually go live the evening of Thanksgiving, so you can shop from home and choose pickup.

Does Target offer early access to Black Friday deals?

Target Circle members get notifications and sometimes early access to select deals. Joining the free program and enabling alerts is the simplest way to shop before the crowds.

What is Target's holiday return policy?

Purchases made during the holiday season are typically returnable through early January, giving you extra time on gifts. Electronics may have a shorter window, so check the receipt.
