How to Land a Funny Wish (Without Crossing a Line)

Great Halloween humour teases situations, not people. Joke about candy economics, costume budgets and universal experiences — everyone laughs because everyone relates. Steer around appearance, fears and anything your friend is genuinely sensitive about, and if you would not say it with them standing beside you, do not send it in a card. One joke per message is plenty; a warm sign-off ("have the best night") turns a gag into a genuine wish.
