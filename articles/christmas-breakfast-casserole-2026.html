Christmas Morning Breakfast Casserole 2026: Make-Ahead Holiday Recipe

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Christmas morning is for presents, not for standing over a stove. A breakfast casserole solves the holiday morning beautifully: assemble it on Christmas Eve, refrigerate overnight, and slide it into the oven while the family opens gifts. By the time the wrapping paper settles, a hot, cheesy, golden breakfast is ready. Here is the classic recipe plus variations to please every crowd in 2026.

The Classic Sausage and Egg Breakfast Casserole

This is the recipe that started it all, and it feeds 8 to 10 people from one 9x13 dish. Brown 1 pound of breakfast sausage, breaking it into crumbles, and drain the fat. Whisk 10 eggs with 2 cups of milk, 1 teaspoon of salt, 1/2 teaspoon of pepper, and 1 teaspoon of dry mustard. Layer 6 cups of cubed day-old bread in a greased baking dish, top with the sausage, 2 cups of shredded cheddar, and diced bell peppers, then pour the egg mixture over everything.

Cover and refrigerate overnight so the bread fully absorbs the custard; this is what gives the casserole its creamy, bread-pudding texture. In the morning, bake uncovered at 350°F for 45 to 55 minutes until puffed, golden, and set in the center. Let it rest 10 minutes before cutting into squares.

Overnight French Toast Casserole

For a sweet Christmas morning, French toast casserole is unbeatable. Cube a loaf of brioche or challah into a greased dish. Whisk 8 eggs with 2 cups of half-and-half, 1/2 cup of brown sugar, 2 teaspoons of cinnamon, and a teaspoon of vanilla, and pour it over the bread. Refrigerate overnight, then bake at 350°F for 40 to 50 minutes.

Top with a brown sugar pecan streusel before baking for a crunchy, caramelized crust, and serve with warm maple syrup and fresh berries. A dusting of powdered sugar makes it look like a snow-covered Christmas morning.

Easy Variations for Every Crowd

The basic formula, bread plus eggs plus milk plus fillings, adapts to any taste:

  • Denver style: Diced ham, bell peppers, and onions with pepper jack cheese for a little kick.
  • Vegetarian: Sautéed mushrooms, spinach, and roasted red peppers with Gruyère or feta.
  • Southwestern: Chorizo, black beans, corn, and pepper jack, served with salsa and sour cream.
  • Caprese: Cherry tomatoes, fresh mozzarella, and basil with a balsamic drizzle after baking.
  • Cinnamon roll: Use cubed cinnamon rolls instead of bread for an ultra-indulgent sweet version.

For a gluten-free crowd, swap the bread for 4 cups of frozen hash browns or tater tots; the result is a crispy-edged hash brown casserole everyone loves.

Make-Ahead and Reheating Guide

Breakfast casseroles are built for the overnight rest, which actually improves the texture. Assemble up to 24 hours ahead and keep refrigerated. If baking straight from the fridge, add 10 to 15 minutes to the bake time. You can also freeze the unbaked casserole for up to 2 months; thaw overnight in the fridge before baking.

Leftovers reheat well: cover individual portions and microwave 1 to 2 minutes, or reheat the whole dish covered at 325°F for 20 minutes. Leftover casserole keeps 3 to 4 days in the fridge and makes an excellent next-day breakfast with a fried egg on top.

Building the Full Christmas Morning Spread

The casserole is the main event, but a few easy sides turn it into a feast. Set out a fruit salad or a bowl of mixed berries, a basket of croissants or muffins, and a pot of coffee plus a pitcher of orange juice. For a festive touch, make a simple cranberry-orange mimosa bar with chilled prosecco. Everything except the coffee can be prepped the night before, keeping Christmas morning calm, delicious, and all about family.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make breakfast casserole the night before?

Yes, that is the whole point. Assemble the casserole on Christmas Eve, refrigerate overnight, and bake in the morning. The overnight rest actually improves the texture.

How long does breakfast casserole bake?

Most 9x13 breakfast casseroles bake 45 to 55 minutes at 350°F. Add 10 to 15 minutes if baking straight from the refrigerator. It is done when puffed, golden, and set in the center.

Can I freeze breakfast casserole?

Yes. Freeze the unbaked assembled casserole for up to 2 months, thaw overnight in the fridge, then bake as directed.

What bread is best for breakfast casserole?

Sturdy, day-old bread works best: sourdough, French bread, brioche, or challah. Avoid soft sandwich bread, which turns mushy.

How do I know when the casserole is done?

The center should be set, not jiggly, and a knife inserted in the middle should come out clean. An instant-read thermometer should read 160°F in the center.
