Galentine's Day Ideas 2027: Celebrate With Friends

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 6 min read

Galentine's Day — February 13, 2027, the day before Valentine's — is the celebration of female friendship: brunch with your best friends, zero romance required. Whether your friends are single, taken, or simply not into Valentine's fuss, a Galentine's get-together is a joyful tradition worth starting. Here are the best ways to celebrate it this year.

Host a Legendary Brunch

Brunch is the classic Galentine's format for a reason: it is daytime, relaxed, and endlessly customizable.

  • Waffle or pancake bar: Keep the base simple and lay out toppings — berries, whipped cream, chocolate chips, maple syrup, Nutella.
  • Mimosa bar: Sparkling wine or cider plus orange, cranberry, and peach juices with fresh fruit for garnish. Label everything with cute tent cards.
  • Heart-shaped food: Use a cookie cutter for heart-shaped toast, pancakes, or fruit slices. Tiny effort, huge charm.
  • Potluck style: Assign each friend a dish so hosting stays fun instead of stressful.

At-Home Spa Day

Turn the living room into a spa with face masks (sheet masks for zero cleanup), a DIY mani-pedi station with polish and hand cream, and hair treatments while you chat. Dim the lights, play a calm playlist, and put phones on silent. Everyone brings their own robe and leaves feeling brand new.

Fun Activities Together

  • Rom-com marathon: Vote on the lineup in advance, build a snack spread, and commit to the full experience.
  • Friendship bracelet making: Embroidery floss and beads — nostalgic, cheap, and everyone leaves with a keepsake.
  • Photo shoot: A simple backdrop, good window light, and a tripod timer. Matching pajamas or pink outfits make it iconic.
  • Karaoke night: A Bluetooth speaker and a lyrics app are all you need for a legendary evening.
  • Vision boards: Magazines, scissors, and poster boards for mapping out the year ahead together.

Thoughtful Gifts and Decor

Set a small gift budget so nobody feels awkward — under $15 keeps it joyful. Good picks: nice lip balm sets, mini candles, friendship bracelets, or a framed group photo from a past adventure. For decor, pink and red balloons, a "Galentine's" banner, and fresh flowers on the table complete the scene without much spending.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Galentine's Day 2027?

Galentine's Day is February 13, 2027 — the day before Valentine's Day. It falls on a Saturday in 2027, which makes it perfect for a brunch or evening gathering.

What do you do on Galentine's Day?

Classic celebrations include brunch, at-home spa days, rom-com marathons, karaoke, and small gift exchanges — anything centered on enjoying your friendships.

Is Galentine's Day only for women?

It started as a celebration of female friendship, but the spirit is really about honoring close friends. Many groups adapt it as a friends' celebration for everyone.

Do you give gifts on Galentine's Day?

Small ones, yes. Keep the budget low — around $10 to $15 — so it stays fun and pressure-free. Thoughtful beats expensive.
