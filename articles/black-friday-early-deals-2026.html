Black Friday Early Deals 2026: Shop the Sales Before the Rush

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 6 min read

Black Friday 2026 falls on November 27, but waiting until the day itself is a rookie mistake. For years now, major retailers have stretched Black Friday into a full season, with genuine discounts appearing as early as late October and building through November. Shopping the early deals means better stock, calmer websites, and often the exact same prices as the big day. Here is how the 2026 early-deal season works and how to grab the best of it before the rush.

When Do Black Friday Early Deals Start?

The early-deal season typically unfolds in waves. The first wave arrives in late October, when Amazon runs its early holiday sale event and competitors quietly match prices on TVs, laptops and toys. The second wave hits in the first two weeks of November: Walmart, Target and Best Buy launch their early Black Friday events, often with "Black Friday prices now" messaging and weekly deal drops. The third wave is Thanksgiving week itself, when prices hit their floor and doorbusters appear. The practical takeaway: start watching prices now, in mid-October, and be ready to buy when your target item hits its historic low rather than waiting for November 27 out of habit.

Where to Find the Best Early Deals

  • Amazon: Early holiday deals on Echo devices, Fire TV sticks, Kindles and Ring products, plus rotating lightning deals on electronics and toys.
  • Walmart: Early Black Friday events with online-first deals; Walmart+ members often get early access windows a few hours before everyone else.
  • Target: Daily and weekly deal drops through November, with strong discounts on toys, small appliances and gift sets.
  • Best Buy: Early tech deals on laptops, TVs, headphones and smart home gear, frequently with open-box options stacked on top.
  • Retailer apps: Download the apps now and enable deal alerts; many early deals are app-exclusive or announced by push notification first.

What Goes on Sale First

Not every category discounts early. TVs and laptops are the classic early movers: retailers use them as traffic magnets, so prices often bottom out two to three weeks before Black Friday. Toys follow a similar pattern, with the hottest toys of the season discounted early to lock in parents before stock runs thin. Small kitchen appliances, air fryers, stand mixers, robot vacuums, also see strong early pricing. What usually waits until the last minute: Apple products (modest discounts, mostly on Thanksgiving week), video game consoles and bundles, and fashion, which peaks closer to the day itself. If your list is tech-heavy, shop early. If it is fashion-heavy, patience pays.

How to Track Prices Like a Pro

The difference between a real deal and a fake one is price history. Use a price tracker such as CamelCamelCamel for Amazon to see an item's price over the past year; if the "deal" matches the price from three months ago, it is not a deal. Keep a simple wishlist with your target price for each item, the lowest price you have ever seen it. Many retailers now offer price adjustment policies: if you buy early and the price drops further within the return window, customer service will often refund the difference, so buying early carries little risk. Screenshot prices when you buy, and check again on Thanksgiving week.

Early Shopping Strategy: The Smart Play

Make your full gift list now and rank items by how likely they are to sell out: hot toys, popular sneaker releases and limited-edition sets first; basics and gift cards last. Buy anything that hits your target price the moment you see it, especially online, because early stock is the deepest stock of the season. Use retailer credit card or loyalty perks for extra cashback, and always check for stackable coupons or cashback portal bonuses before checkout. Finally, remember that Cyber Monday, November 30 this year, often beats Black Friday on laptops, small electronics and fashion, so leave those categories for the second wave if prices look flat.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27, the day after Thanksgiving. Cyber Monday follows on November 30.

Are early Black Friday deals as good as the day itself?

Often yes. Retailers now run "Black Friday prices now" events weeks early, and price adjustment policies mean buying early rarely costs you. Stock is also better before the rush.

What sells out first during early deals?

Hot toys of the season, popular tech at deep discounts, and limited-edition items. Buy these the moment they hit your target price.

How do I know if an early deal is real?

Check the price history with a tracker like CamelCamelCamel. A real deal beats the item's average price over the past six to twelve months, not just its inflated "list price."

Should I wait for Cyber Monday instead?

For laptops, small electronics and fashion, Cyber Monday often has the edge. For TVs, toys and big-ticket items, Black Friday week is usually the floor.
