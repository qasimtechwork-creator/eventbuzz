St. Patrick's Day 2027: Celebration Guide & Party Ideas

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 3 min read

St. Patrick's Day 2027 falls on Wednesday, March 17, 2027. What began as a feast day honoring Ireland's patron saint has grown into a worldwide celebration of Irish culture, complete with parades, music, green everything and a well-poured pint. Whether you are Irish or just Irish for the day, this guide covers the story behind the holiday and how to celebrate it properly.

The Story Behind the Day

Saint Patrick was a 5th-century missionary credited with bringing Christianity to Ireland. Legend says he used the three-leafed shamrock to explain the Holy Trinity, which is why the little plant became the symbol of the day. Ireland's nickname, the Emerald Isle, comes from its famously green landscape, and the color green became tied to Irish identity centuries ago. The first St. Patrick's Day parade was not held in Ireland at all but in New York City in 1762, organized by Irish soldiers serving in the British army. Today the holiday is celebrated in more countries than any other national festival.

Party Ideas and Games

A St. Patrick's Day party works at any scale. Keep it simple with an Irish music playlist, green decorations and a few themed snacks, or go bigger with a full pub-style gathering. Fun games include Irish trivia, a "pin the hat on the leprechaun" twist on the classic party game, and a scavenger hunt for gold coins hidden around the house. If your city hosts a parade, make a morning of it before the party. For a midweek 2027 celebration, a low-key evening gathering with close friends often beats an elaborate plan.

Food and Drink

Traditional Irish-American fare centers on corned beef and cabbage, best cooked low and slow until tender. Shepherd's pie, soda bread with good butter and colcannon, a comforting mash of potatoes and cabbage, round out the classics. For drinks, Irish stout is the obvious choice, but Irish coffee and a well-made whiskey cocktail suit the evening too. End with something sweet and green: pistachio cupcakes, mint chocolate treats or a shamrock-shaped sugar cookie. A pot of strong tea keeps the non-drinkers happily in the spirit.

What to Wear

The tradition of wearing green comes with a playful rule: legend says leprechauns pinch anyone they can see, and wearing green makes you invisible to them. In 2027, lean into the theme without looking like a costume: an emerald dress, a green knit, or green accessories like a scarf, socks or a clover pin all do the job. A touch of gold jewelry nods to the pot of gold. Whatever you wear, wear the green proudly, because on March 17, everyone is a little bit Irish.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is St. Patrick's Day 2027?

St. Patrick's Day 2027 falls on Wednesday, March 17, 2027. The date is the same every year, honoring the traditional death date of Saint Patrick.

Why do people wear green on St. Patrick's Day?

Green is tied to Ireland, the Emerald Isle, and to the shamrock. Folklore adds a playful reason: wearing green makes you invisible to mischievous leprechauns who would otherwise pinch you.

What does "Erin go Bragh" mean?

It is an anglicization of the Irish phrase meaning "Ireland forever," a traditional expression of Irish pride often seen on St. Patrick's Day.

Is St. Patrick's Day a public holiday?

It is a public holiday in Ireland and Northern Ireland. In the United States it is not a federal holiday, but it is widely celebrated with parades and parties in cities across the country.
