Valentine's Crafts for Kids 2027: Fun & Easy Projects

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 5 min read

February is craft season in every household with kids, and Valentine's Day is the sweetest excuse of all. These Valentine's crafts for kids are genuinely easy, use supplies you probably already own, and produce things grandparents will actually want to keep. Most projects need nothing fancier than paper, crayons, glue, and a little glitter. Pick one project per sitting, keep sessions under 45 minutes, and remember: the goal is fun, not perfection.

Paper Heart Cards and Garlands

The classics endure for a reason. Folded paper hearts take two minutes to teach and make great cards when kids add a drawing and a name inside. Chain them into garlands with yarn and tape for instant February decor across a doorway or mantel. Older kids can try woven paper hearts, a traditional Scandinavian craft that looks impressive and hides small candies inside. A simple heart template printed from any search engine keeps shapes neat for younger cutters, and mixing red, pink, and white paper gives the garland a polished look.

Fingerprint and Handprint Keepsakes

Little fingerprints become heart shapes, flowers, and love bugs with surprising ease. A thumbprint heart card is a grandparent magnet, and adding the date and the child's age turns it into a time capsule. Salt-dough handprint hearts, baked and painted, become ornaments that get pulled out every February for years. Seal finished pieces with clear varnish so the paint survives little hands and years of February displays. Washable paint is non-negotiable here.

Classroom Valentine Exchanges

If your child is exchanging valentines at school, homemade beats store-bought every time. Simple folded cards with a heart sticker and their name take minutes each, and teachers appreciate when they are easy to hand out. Set up a little assembly line: one child folds, another sticks, another signs. Add a small treat only if the school allows it, and always check the class allergy list before including food.

Age-by-Age Tips

Toddlers do best with stickers, stamps, and pre-cut shapes glued onto card stock. Preschoolers can cut simple hearts with safety scissors and handle basic folding. School-age kids can manage weaving, simple origami hearts, and writing their own messages. Teens might roll their eyes at paper hearts but often secretly enjoy making elaborate cards for friends or a crush. Meet them where they are, let them lead, and keep a few extra supplies on hand for the friends who inevitably wander over to join in.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are easy Valentine's crafts for toddlers?

Stick with stickers, heart stamps, pre-cut shapes, and washable paint handprints. Keep sessions short, around 15 to 20 minutes.

How do we make Valentine's crafts without a mess?

Use a washable tablecloth or tray, stick to glue sticks and stickers instead of liquid glue and paint, and keep wipes within reach.

What should kids write in handmade valentines?

Simple is best: their name, a heart drawing, and one kind sentence like 'You are a great friend.' Younger kids can dictate to an adult.

When should we start crafting for Valentine's Day?

The first week of February gives plenty of time for a project or two plus classroom cards, without the last-minute rush.
