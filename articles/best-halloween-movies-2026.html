Best Halloween Movies 2026: Scary, Funny & Family Favorites to Watch

Published October 9, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Every Halloween has two soundtracks: the doorbell for trick-or-treaters, and a great movie playing behind it. For Halloween 2026 — Saturday, October 31 — we have sorted the best Halloween movies to watch by the only question that matters: how scared do you actually want to be? Family favorites first, then funny, then the classic and modern horror for when the kids have gone to bed.

Every pick below notes who it suits, so mixed groups can plan a two-film night: something gentle at seven, something genuinely frightening at ten.

Family Favorites (Spooky, Not Scary)

Hocus Pocus (1993)

Three resurrected witch sisters cause chaos in modern Salem. Funny-witchy, endlessly quotable, and the definitive family Halloween film for roughly three decades of viewers. Suits: ages 7+ with parents, and every nostalgic adult.

The Nightmare Before Christmas (1993)

The Pumpkin King of Halloween Town tries to take over Christmas. Gorgeous stop-motion, unforgettable songs, and a hero who is scary-looking and sweet underneath. Suits: all ages; the perfect bridge between Halloween and Christmas season.

Casper (1995)

The friendly ghost gets his own gentle story about friendship and kindness. Suits: younger kids (5+) who want a ghost they can hug.

Halloweentown (1998)

A young girl discovers her family of witches and a town where it is Halloween all year. Suits: kids 6–11 and families building a yearly tradition.

Coraline (2009)

A brave girl finds a door to an "Other" world that is almost perfect... almost. Beautiful and genuinely eerie — the bravest of the family picks. Suits: ages 9+, and adults who will discuss it for days.

Hotel Transylvania (2012)

Dracula runs a hotel for monsters and panics when a human arrives. Pure comedy chaos in a monster wrapper. Suits: ages 5+ and anyone who wants zero fear.

ParaNorman (2012)

A boy who can talk to ghosts becomes the only hope of a town cursed by a witch's trial. Funny, touching and beautifully animated, with a climax that is more emotional than frightening. Suits: ages 8+, and parents who cry at cartoons (you know who you are).

It's the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown (1966)

The gentle classic: Linus waits faithfully in the pumpkin patch while the gang trick-or-treats. At under half an hour, it is the perfect opening act for the youngest viewers before the main feature. Suits: ages 4+, and every household starting a tradition.

Funny Halloween Movies (Laughter First, Screams Second)

Ghostbusters (1984)

Four scientists-turned-ghost-removers save New York with deadpan comedy. Suits: families with older kids and every adult who has never seen it (fix that this year).

Beetlejuice (1988)

A chaotic ghost-for-hire turns a haunted house into surreal comedy. Suits: teens and adults who like their Halloween weird and stylish.

The Addams Family (1991)

The spookiest family in film history, played completely straight — which is exactly why it is so funny. Suits: ages 8+ and mixed groups.

Classic Horror (For Horror Fans)

Halloween (1978)

Michael Myers escapes and returns to his hometown on Halloween night. The original slasher blueprint — pure suspense, an iconic score, and the reason this list exists. Suits: adults; the connoisseur's Halloween-night screening.

A Nightmare on Elm Street (1984)

A killer who attacks in dreams means nobody is safe asleep. Imaginative, strange and still unsettling. Suits: adults and horror completists.

Scream (1996)

A masked killer terrorises a town of horror-movie experts, and the film has enormous fun with the rules of the genre. Suits: adults and groups who like to shout advice at the screen.

Modern Scary (Recent Decades, High Tension)

Get Out (2017)

A weekend meeting his girlfriend's parents slides into a nightmare of manners and dread. Smart, tense, and rewatchable. Suits: adults who prefer psychological fear.

A Quiet Place (2018)

A family survives in silence because the monsters hunt by sound. Almost no dialogue, maximum tension — watching it in a quiet room is an experience in itself. Suits: teens (with parents) and adults.

It (2017)

A group of kids face a shape-shifting evil that often wears a clown's face. Scary, but also a real story about friendship. Suits: adults and older teens.

How to Plan Your 2026 Movie Night

  • Two-film rule: family pick at 7 p.m., horror pick after the youngest viewers leave or sleep. Nobody has to choose between the two Halloweens.
  • Vote with a poll: send three posters to the group chat and let the majority pick; losers get first choice of snacks.
  • Snacks are decor: popcorn in pumpkin bowls, labelled drinks from our recipes guide, and a blanket per person — horror is colder than you think.
  • Lights plan: low but never zero for family films; true darkness only for the adult feature.
  • End gentle: finish the night with a funny scene or a cartoon short, especially with kids — the last five minutes decide the dreams.

A Word on Ratings and Rewatching

Every film above carries its own official age rating in your country, and ratings exist for good reasons — check the certificate on your copy or streaming page before promising a film to younger viewers. Scare tolerance also varies wildly between children of the same age; the child who laughed through Coraline may still be undone by a single clown. When in doubt, preview the first ten minutes yourself, and remember that a rewatch of a beloved film counts as a Halloween tradition, not a failure of imagination. Many families find their "one true Halloween movie" within three years and guard the ritual fiercely — you are allowed to be those people.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Halloween movie for young kids?

Casper (1995) and Hotel Transylvania (2012) are the gentlest starting points: ghosts and monsters played entirely for warmth and comedy.

What is the scariest movie on this list?

Halloween (1978) for pure sustained suspense, and It (2017) for modern shock — both firmly adult viewing.

Which Halloween movies are funny rather than scary?

Ghostbusters (1984), Beetlejuice (1988) and The Addams Family (1991) deliver the Halloween atmosphere with laughs in the lead role.

What should a mixed group of adults and kids watch?

Hocus Pocus (1993) is the great compromise: witches, jokes and a little danger that plays happily to both ends of the sofa. Follow with Coraline (2009) if the older kids want more.

When is the best time to watch Halloween movies in 2026?

Build up through the last week of October and hold your marathon on Halloween night itself, Saturday, October 31, 2026 — after trick-or-treating, when the house goes quiet.

Conclusion

Pick the scare level honestly, stack the snacks within reach, and let one great film carry the night. Whether your 2026 ends with a friendly ghost or a masked legend, a shared movie is the warmest haunted house there is.
