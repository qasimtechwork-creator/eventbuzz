When Is Thanksgiving 2026: Date, History & Why We Celebrate

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Thanksgiving is the holiday built around a table, a pause, and the words "thank you" said out loud. If you are planning travel, booking time off, or simply counting down to the feast, this guide gives you the 2026 date, the short story of how the holiday began, and how families celebrate it today.

Quick answer: Thanksgiving Day 2026 falls on Thursday, November 26, 2026. In the United States, Thanksgiving is always the fourth Thursday of November, so the 2026 date sits near the end of the month. Most families gather for the main meal in the afternoon or early evening of November 26.

The Date at a Glance

  • Thanksgiving Day: Thursday, November 26, 2026
  • The rule: The fourth Thursday of November, every year
  • The day after: Friday, November 27, widely known as Black Friday, when the Christmas shopping season opens
  • The long weekend: Many schools and offices close on both Thursday and Friday, making it a four-day break
  • Canadian Thanksgiving: Monday, October 12, 2026 (the second Monday of October)

Because the date moves between November 22 and November 28 each year, 2026 gives hosts a little extra time after Halloween to plan the menu and the guest list.

A Short History of Thanksgiving

The holiday traces back to a harvest feast in 1621 at Plymouth, where English settlers and members of the Wampanoag people shared a meal after the colony's first successful corn harvest. Harvest thanksgiving meals were older than that gathering, but the 1621 feast became the story the modern holiday grew from.

In 1789, George Washington issued the first national Thanksgiving proclamation, asking Americans to set aside a day of public thanksgiving and gratitude. Through the 1800s, individual states kept their own dates, and writer Sarah Josepha Hale campaigned for nearly four decades for one shared national day. In 1863, in the middle of the Civil War, Abraham Lincoln proclaimed a national Thanksgiving Day on the last Thursday of November, writing that the year had "been filled with the blessings of fruitful fields and healthful skies."

The date shifted one last time in the 20th century. After Franklin D. Roosevelt moved the holiday a week earlier in 1939 to lengthen the Christmas shopping season, public confusion followed, and in 1941 Congress fixed Thanksgiving permanently as the fourth Thursday of November — the rule that gives us Thursday, November 26 in 2026.

Why We Celebrate

Underneath the food, Thanksgiving is a gratitude ritual. The harvest roots matter — the holiday arrives when fields are gathered in and storehouses are full — but most families today celebrate it as a day to count what went right: health, work, friendship, people who showed up when it mattered. Many tables still open the meal with each person naming one thing they are thankful for, a small tradition that costs nothing and changes the mood of the whole day.

It is also one of the few holidays with almost no gifts attached. The celebration is the gathering itself, which is exactly why so many people travel across the country for it.

  • The gratitude moment: A toast, a prayer, or a simple round of thank-yous before anyone picks up a fork.
  • Giving back: Many families volunteer at food banks or invite a neighbour, colleague or student who would otherwise spend the day alone.

Thanksgiving Beyond the United States

Canada celebrates Thanksgiving on the second Monday of October — Monday, October 12 in 2026 — with the same harvest spirit and a similar table. Several other countries hold harvest or gratitude festivals in autumn under different names, which is a reminder that pausing to give thanks at the end of the growing season is one of humanity's oldest shared ideas.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving Day 2026 is Thursday, November 26, 2026 in the United States. It always falls on the fourth Thursday of November.

Why is Thanksgiving on a Thursday?

The Thursday tradition dates to Abraham Lincoln's 1863 proclamation of a national day of thanksgiving on the last Thursday of November. Congress settled the modern rule in 1941, fixing the holiday on the fourth Thursday of November.

Is Thanksgiving a public holiday?

Yes. Thanksgiving is a federal holiday in the United States, so government offices, banks and most schools close, and many businesses shut for the day. Many workplaces also close on the Friday that follows.

How many days are left until Thanksgiving 2026?

From October 10, 2026, there are 47 days until Thanksgiving Day. That is plenty of time to plan travel, test a new side dish, and send your invitations.

What is the difference between US and Canadian Thanksgiving?

Both are harvest gratitude holidays with a similar feast. The main difference is timing: Canada celebrates on the second Monday of October (October 12, 2026), about six weeks earlier than the American date, reflecting its earlier northern harvest.

Conclusion

Mark the date now: Thursday, November 26, 2026. Whether your Thanksgiving is a loud family reunion, a small dinner with friends, or a quiet day of rest, a little planning turns it from hectic to heartfelt. Start with the menu, send your messages early, and leave room at the table — and in the schedule — for gratitude.
