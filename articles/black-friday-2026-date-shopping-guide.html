Black Friday 2026: Date, Store Hours & Smart Shopping Guide

DRAFT — not published yet · Prepared October 12, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Black Friday 2026 falls on Friday, November 27, 2026, the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). It remains the biggest single shopping day of the holiday season, with retailers cutting prices across electronics, toys, apparel, appliances and home goods. This guide covers the confirmed dates, typical store hours, how deals actually work in 2026, and a smart plan so you buy the right things at the right prices.

Quick answer: Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27. Most major stores open early Friday morning; many stay closed on Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26), with online deals going live Thursday evening or earlier. The strongest discounts usually land on TVs, toys, apparel and appliances — always compare against the price from a few weeks earlier before calling a deal a deal.

When Is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is always the day after Thanksgiving in the United States, and Thanksgiving falls on the fourth Thursday of November. In 2026 that fixes the whole holiday shopping calendar:

  • Thanksgiving (US): Thursday, November 26, 2026
  • Black Friday: Friday, November 27, 2026
  • Small Business Saturday: Saturday, November 28, 2026
  • Cyber Monday: Monday, November 30, 2026
  • Christmas Day: Friday, December 25, 2026

The four days from Black Friday to Cyber Monday are often called Cyber Week, and many retailers now treat the whole stretch — plus the weeks before it — as one long sale. The date itself still matters: it is when the deepest, most advertised price cuts land.

Black Friday Store Hours: What to Expect

Store hours follow a pattern that has held for several years now. Most large chains keep their doors closed on Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26, 2026), shifting the Thursday action to their websites and apps, where sales typically go live Thursday evening. On Black Friday itself, doors usually open early in the morning — historically anywhere from 5 AM to 7 AM for the big chains — with the most aggressive doorbuster offers reserved for the first hours.

Because hours vary by retailer and by location, check your specific store's posted hours the week before. If you are shopping online, the exact opening hour matters less: most web deals run for the full day, and many start the evening before. For popular items with limited stock, earlier is still safer.

How Black Friday Deals Work in 2026

The honest truth first: Black Friday is no longer a single day. Large retailers now spread holiday discounts across October and November, with early deal events running weeks before Thanksgiving. That means the price you see on Friday, November 27 may have been available — at the same level — days or weeks earlier.

What still makes the day itself special is concentration: the widest range of discounts is live at the same time, which makes it the best day to comparison-shop a researched shortlist. A practical approach is to track the prices of the items you want from early November onward. If a "Black Friday deal" matches the price the item carried in September, it is not a deal — it is marketing. Real deals show a genuine drop from the item's recent selling price.

A Smart Shopping Plan for Black Friday

  1. Make a list now. Write down exactly what you plan to buy, with acceptable price targets. A list is the single best defense against impulse spending.
  2. Set a total budget. Decide the maximum you will spend across the whole season, not per store. Black Friday, Cyber Monday and Christmas shopping come from the same wallet.
  3. Track prices early. Note current prices for your shortlist in early November so you can recognize a genuine cut when you see one.
  4. Check price-match policies. Several major retailers match competitors' advertised prices and their own later markdowns for a limited window — read the current terms before you buy.
  5. Know the return policy. Holiday return windows are usually extended, but exclusions exist on electronics and opened items. Confirm before big-ticket purchases.
  6. Factor in the real total. Compare the delivered price: item price plus shipping, minus coupons and cashback. Store pickup can erase delivery fees on bulky items.

What to Buy on Black Friday vs Cyber Monday

Industry forecasts for 2026 point to a rough split. Black Friday tends to carry the deepest cuts on TVs, toys, apparel and appliances, with the added option of seeing big items in person and taking them home the same day. Cyber Monday, on Monday, November 30, leans toward electronics and computers, with peak online discounts in those categories.

Treat this as a starting guide, not a guarantee: an individual item can be cheapest on either day, and limited stock sells out fast. If there is one specific must-have item, buy it when you see a price you are happy with rather than gambling on a better one later.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday 2026 is Friday, November 27, 2026 — the day after Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26). Cyber Monday follows on Monday, November 30.

Is Black Friday always on the same date?

No. It is always the day after US Thanksgiving, which falls on the fourth Thursday of November, so the calendar date shifts each year.

Are stores open on Thanksgiving 2026?

In recent years most major US chains have kept stores closed on Thanksgiving Day, with the Thursday sales happening online. Confirm your local store's hours the week before, as policies can change.

Do Black Friday deals start before Friday?

Yes. Many retailers run early Black Friday promotions for days or weeks beforehand. Track the prices of items you want from early November so you can tell a real markdown from a relabeled one.

What is usually cheapest on Black Friday?

TVs, toys, apparel and appliances typically see their deepest discounts on Black Friday, while electronics and computers often peak on Cyber Monday. Compare the specific item rather than trusting the category rule blindly.

Conclusion

Circle Friday, November 27 on the calendar, make your list and set your budget now, and track prices through November. Shoppers who arrive with a plan get the real deals; shoppers who arrive without one get the marketing. Happy bargain hunting.
