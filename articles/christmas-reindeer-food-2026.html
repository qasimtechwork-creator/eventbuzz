Reindeer Food Recipe & Christmas Eve Traditions 2026

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Sprinkling reindeer food on the lawn on Christmas Eve is one of those small rituals kids remember forever. The idea is simple: Santa's reindeer need fuel for their long night, so children scatter a sparkly mix of oats on the doorstep to guide them in. It takes five minutes to make, costs almost nothing, and pairs perfectly with leaving out cookies for Santa. Here is the classic recipe, plus variations and packaging ideas for 2026.

The Classic Reindeer Food Recipe

The traditional mix needs only three things: rolled oats, sugar, and something sparkly. Combine about half a cup of oats with two tablespoons of sugar and a teaspoon of edible glitter or colored sprinkles. The sugar gives it a frosty shimmer that children love, and the oats are the actual reindeer fuel of the story. Mix it in a bowl and let the kids do the stirring; making it together is half the fun. That is the entire recipe. Anything fancier is optional.

Fun Variations

Once you know the base, you can customize it. Cocoa powder turns it into chocolate reindeer food. A drop of vanilla extract makes it smell like a bakery. Cinnamon adds a warm holiday scent that kids notice the moment the bowl is opened. For a colorful version, divide the mix and tint portions with a drop of food coloring before adding sprinkles. Keep every ingredient edible and pet-safe, since the mix will end up on the lawn where animals might find it.

Packaging and Presentation

Presentation is where reindeer food shines as a craft. Spoon portions into small cellophane bags and tie them with ribbon. Attach a tag with a short poem: many families use the classic rhyme about sprinkling the food on the lawn so the reindeer can find their home. These little bags make wonderful classroom gifts, party favors or neighbor treats in the week before Christmas. Let the kids sign the tags themselves; imperfect handwriting makes them even more charming.

More Christmas Eve Traditions to Try

Reindeer food works best as part of a bigger Christmas Eve ritual. The classic pairing is cookies and milk for Santa with a carrot for the reindeer. Many families also track Santa's journey online after dinner, read a Christmas story aloud, or hang a special ornament together. The common thread is giving kids something active to do on a night when excitement makes sitting still impossible. Pick two or three traditions and repeat them yearly; repetition is what turns a fun evening into a family ritual.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is reindeer food safe for real animals?

Keep it simple and edible: oats, sugar and food-safe sprinkles are fine if a bird or pet finds a bit. Avoid craft glitter, which is plastic and should never be scattered outside. Edible glitter gives the same sparkle.

Can you eat reindeer food?

Yes, the classic mix is just oats, sugar and sprinkles, so it is edible, though not exactly delicious. Kids sometimes taste it during the making, which is part of the fun.

How much reindeer food do you sprinkle?

A small handful per child is plenty. The tradition is symbolic, so a light scattering across the doorstep or lawn is all it takes.

What do you leave for Santa alongside reindeer food?

The classic setup is cookies and a glass of milk for Santa, plus a carrot for the reindeer. Many families add a thank-you note, which kids love writing.
