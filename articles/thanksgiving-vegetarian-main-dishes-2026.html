15 Vegetarian Thanksgiving Main Dishes 2026: Hearty Meatless Centerpieces

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Every vegetarian at a Thanksgiving table knows the plate: a mountain of sides and an empty space where the turkey should be. This year, give the vegetarians at your table, including yourself, a real centerpiece. These 15 vegetarian Thanksgiving main dishes are hearty, festive, and substantial enough to anchor a plate, from dramatic whole roasted squash to savory pastries that make meat-eaters ask for a slice.

Showstopping Roasted Centerpieces

A whole roasted cauliflower with a spiced yogurt or tahini glaze, roasted at 400 degrees for 45 to 55 minutes, slices like a roast and looks magnificent on a platter. Stuffed butternut squash, halved and filled with a wild rice, cranberry, pecan, and sage mixture, is the quintessential vegetarian Thanksgiving main: autumnal, colorful, and completely self-contained. For maximum drama, roast a whole pumpkin stuffed with a savory bread pudding of mushrooms, gruyere, and herbs; slice it at the table like a pie. A glazed acorn squash with maple and miso, roasted cut-side down until caramelized, makes an elegant individual portion for each guest.

Savory Pastries and Wellingtons

A mushroom Wellington is the vegetarian answer to a holiday roast: a duxelles of finely chopped mushrooms, shallots, and thyme wrapped in puff pastry with a layer of spinach and goat cheese, baked at 400 degrees until deep golden. Individual beet and goat cheese Wellingtons, with roasted beets and caramelized onions in flaky pastry, are stunning and easy to portion. A butternut squash and sage galette, free-form on a baking sheet, feeds a crowd with rustic charm. For something simpler, a wild mushroom and leek pot pie with a biscuit topping delivers all the comfort with half the effort.

Hearty Loaves and Bakes

  • Lentil loaf: Brown lentils, oats, walnuts, onion, and mushrooms baked in a loaf pan at 375 for 50 minutes, glazed with a maple-tomato topping. Sliceable, savory, and great with gravy.
  • Stuffed portobello mushrooms: Large caps filled with herbed breadcrumbs, parmesan, and spinach, roasted at 375 for 20 minutes.
  • Eggplant parmesan bake: Layers of breaded eggplant, marinara, and mozzarella, baked until bubbly. A crowd-pleaser that scales easily.
  • Sweet potato and black bean enchiladas: Rolled tortillas with roasted sweet potato and black beans in enchilada sauce, topped with cheese and baked 25 minutes.
  • Butternut squash lasagna: Roasted squash puree, ricotta, sage, and mozzarella layered with noodles. Rich, festive, and make-ahead friendly.

Grains, Gratins, and Skillet Mains

A wild rice and mushroom casserole with gruyere and crispy shallots eats like a main dish when served in generous portions. A creamy polenta topped with roasted mushrooms, thyme, and parmesan is rustic luxury in a bowl. For the table, a farro and roasted vegetable platter with pomegranate seeds and a sherry vinaigrette brings color and chew. And a spanakopita-style spinach and feta pie in a round pan, cut into wedges, looks like it belongs at a holiday feast.

Serving Vegetarians Well

A few hosting notes make all the difference. Cook the vegetarian main in a separate pan from any meat, and use vegetable broth in the dishes meant for vegetarian guests. Label dishes clearly so guests do not have to ask. Make the vegetarian main in a quantity that allows seconds; vegetarians are used to being an afterthought, and abundance is the kindest gesture. Serve it at the same time as the turkey, on a platter, carved or sliced at the table, so it feels like a centerpiece rather than a side note. When the whole table reaches for it, you will know you chose right.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best vegetarian main dish for Thanksgiving?

A mushroom Wellington or a stuffed whole butternut squash are the most popular choices: both look dramatic, slice beautifully, and satisfy vegetarians and meat-eaters alike.

How do I make sure vegetarian dishes are truly vegetarian?

Use vegetable broth instead of chicken or turkey broth, cook in separate pans from meat, and check that cheeses do not contain animal rennet if your guests are strict vegetarians. Label dishes clearly.

Can I make vegetarian Thanksgiving mains ahead of time?

Yes. Lentil loaf, lasagna, and gratins can be assembled a day ahead and refrigerated, then baked on Thanksgiving. Stuffed squash can be prepped the night before. Wellingtons are best baked fresh but the filling can be made ahead.

What can vegetarians eat at Thanksgiving besides sides?

Beyond the mains in this guide, most classic sides are vegetarian-friendly: mashed potatoes, green bean casserole, cranberry sauce, dinner rolls, salads, and most pies. Just confirm the broth and butter situation with the cook.

How much vegetarian main dish should I make?

Plan one generous serving per vegetarian guest plus extra for seconds and curious meat-eaters. A good rule: make enough for the vegetarians plus about a third more, since these dishes tend to disappear.
