Easter 2027 Prep Guide: Everything You Need to Know

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 3 min read

Easter 2027 falls on Sunday, March 28, 2027, one of the earliest possible dates for the holiday, and it arrives just as spring is finding its footing. For Christians, Easter celebrates the resurrection of Jesus Christ and stands as the most important day in the Christian calendar. For everyone else, it has become a joyful spring celebration built around painted eggs, chocolate bunnies, family brunches and egg hunts on the lawn. Because Easter moves every year, a little advance planning makes all the difference. This guide covers the key dates of Holy Week 2027, how to plan a memorable egg hunt, an easy Easter brunch menu, decoration ideas and the traditions that make the day special.

Key Dates: Holy Week 2027

Easter Sunday lands on March 28, 2027, which sets the whole week of observances around it. Palm Sunday is March 21, Maundy Thursday is March 25 and Good Friday is March 26. Because Easter 2027 comes so early, spring weather may still be chilly in many regions, so plan outdoor events with a warm backup in mind. Many families attend sunrise services that begin just as the light returns, while others mark Good Friday with quiet reflection before the celebration of Sunday. If you travel for the holiday, book flights and accommodation early, since this is one of the busiest weekends of the spring season.

Planning the Perfect Egg Hunt

The egg hunt is the centerpiece of Easter morning for kids, and a great one takes only a little preparation. Hide eggs at different heights and difficulty levels so toddlers and older children each get their fair share of finds. Use plastic refillable eggs filled with small treats, stickers or coins rather than chocolate that melts in the sun. Set clear boundaries before the hunt begins and give each child a basket in a different color so nothing gets mixed up. For a twist that older kids love, turn the hunt into a scavenger hunt with rhyming clues leading from egg to egg and ending at a golden egg with a special prize. Number the eggs if you want to keep things fair, and keep a list of your hiding spots so no egg goes undiscovered until July.

An Easy Easter Brunch Menu

Easter brunch should feel festive without keeping the cook trapped in the kitchen all morning. A make-ahead strata or baked French toast casserole can go straight into the oven when you wake up. Add a glazed ham or honey-baked spiral ham as the centerpiece, with roasted asparagus and spring peas on the side to nod at the season. Deviled eggs are a natural fit, and hot cross buns carry the holiday's symbolism in every bite. For dessert, keep it simple: a lemon drizzle cake or a carrot cake with cream cheese frosting never fails. Set the table the night before with fresh flowers, pastel napkins and a few chocolate eggs scattered along the runner, and brunch practically hosts itself.

Decorations and Traditions

Easter decorating leans on the colors of new life: soft yellows, pinks, greens and lavenders. Fill a vase with flowering branches or tulips for an instant centerpiece, and display painted eggs in a glass bowl or hung from branches for charming, inexpensive decor. If you observe the religious side of Easter, many families decorate with lilies, attend church services and share the traditional greeting of the day. Whatever your traditions, the heart of Easter is renewal: gathering the people you love and marking the turn of the season together. Start your preparations a week or two before March 28, and Easter 2027 will feel joyful rather than rushed.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Easter 2027?

Easter Sunday 2027 falls on March 28, 2027. It is one of the earliest possible dates for Easter, which can fall anywhere between March 22 and April 25.

Why does the date of Easter change every year?

Easter is a moveable feast. It is set for the first Sunday after the first full moon on or after the spring equinox, which is why the date shifts each year.

What is traditionally eaten on Easter?

Classic Easter foods include glazed ham or roast lamb, deviled eggs, hot cross buns, spring vegetables like asparagus and peas, and carrot cake or lemon cake for dessert.

How can I dye Easter eggs with natural ingredients?

Simmer eggs with onion skins for gold, red cabbage for blue, beets for pink or turmeric for yellow. Add a splash of vinegar to help the color set, then rub with a little oil for shine.
