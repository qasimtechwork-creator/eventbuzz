Spring Break Travel Guide 2027: Best Destinations

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 3 min read

Spring break 2027 is the perfect excuse to shake off winter. Whether you want beaches, cities or mountains, traveling in March and April means shoulder-season prices and milder crowds if you plan smart. This guide rounds up the best destinations, money-saving booking strategies and practical tips for a smooth trip.

Best Destinations for Spring Break 2027

Beach lovers have the classic options: Florida's Gulf Coast, Cancun and the Caribbean offer warm water and reliable sunshine, while Southern California pairs beaches with city life. Prefer cities? New Orleans, Austin and San Diego shine in spring weather without summer crowds. For nature, the desert Southwest and national parks like Zion and Joshua Tree are at their best in March and April, warm days, cool nights, wildflowers in bloom. Internationally, consider Portugal, Greece or Morocco for spring value before peak season prices arrive.

Booking Smart and Saving Money

Timing is everything. Book flights two to three months ahead for the best fares, and fly midweek when prices dip. Compare vacation rentals and hostels against hotels, especially for groups where splitting a rental beats multiple rooms. Bundle flights and accommodation when the package genuinely saves money, but price the pieces separately first. Travel insurance is worth it for international or expensive trips: medical coverage abroad alone justifies the cost. Set price alerts early and be flexible on exact dates; shifting by a few days can cut the fare dramatically.

Packing and Planning

Pack for versatility, not volume. A capsule of mix-and-match pieces in breathable fabrics covers beach days and nice dinners, and layers handle cool evenings. A good backpack works as a personal item and a day bag at your destination. Keep documents digital and backed up: photos of your passport, bookings and insurance in your phone and email. Build an itinerary with breathing room, one big activity per day beats a frantic checklist. And leave copies of your plans with someone at home.

Staying Safe

A little caution goes a long way. Share your itinerary with someone you trust and check in regularly. Keep valuables in the hotel safe and carry only what you need for the day. Stay aware after dark, stick to well-lit areas and use licensed transport. Learn the local emergency number before you go, it is not 911 everywhere. Drink responsibly, watch your drinks in crowded nightlife areas, and trust your instincts: if a situation feels wrong, leave. The goal is memories, not mishaps.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is spring break 2027?

Most US schools schedule spring break sometime between mid-March and early April 2027, but exact dates vary by school and district, so check your own calendar first.

What are the cheapest spring break destinations?

Domestic beach towns, Mexico, and off-peak cities generally offer the best value. Traveling midweek and avoiding the single busiest week of mid-March cuts costs further.

How far in advance should I book spring break travel?

Book flights two to three months ahead, and reserve accommodation even earlier for popular beach destinations that sell out.

Is travel insurance worth it for spring break?

For international trips or expensive bookings, yes. Medical coverage abroad and trip cancellation protection alone justify the relatively small cost.
