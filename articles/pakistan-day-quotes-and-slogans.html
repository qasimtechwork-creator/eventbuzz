Pakistan Day Quotes & Slogans: Patriotic Lines for 23 March

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Every 23 March, the right words matter. A line from the Quaid can open a school speech with instant gravity, and a short slogan on a banner can carry a whole ceremony. This page brings together famous public sayings associated with Muhammad Ali Jinnah, widely quoted and taught in classrooms, alongside original patriotic lines and slogans written fresh for Pakistan Day 2027, ready for speeches, captions and school functions. If you need the story behind the day first, read our Pakistan Day history and Lahore Resolution guide. In 2027, Pakistan Day falls on Tuesday, March 23.

Quick answer: The most famous Pakistan Day quote is Jinnah's guiding motto, "Faith, Unity, Discipline." For a school banner, the favourite short slogans are "Pakistan Zindabad" and "23 March: The Day a Dream Took Shape."

Famous Sayings of Quaid-e-Azam

These short, well-known public remarks of Muhammad Ali Jinnah are in the public domain of history, taught in schools and quoted every 23 March. Keep them short in speeches; one or two lines quoted well are stronger than a long passage.

Faith, Unity, Discipline.
There is no power on earth that can undo Pakistan.
With faith, discipline and selfless devotion to duty, there is nothing worthwhile that you cannot achieve.
Work, work and only work.
No nation can rise to the height of glory unless your women are side by side with you.

Pair a quotation with one sentence of your own about what it means today, for your school, your city or your family. That small bridge between a historic voice and the present audience is what makes a quotation land.

How to Use These Quotes Well

For a speech, open with one famous saying, tell one short fact about 23 March 1940, then close with a slogan the audience can repeat after you. For a banner or chart, choose slogans of five words or fewer so the letters can stay large and readable. For captions, one patriotic line plus one personal sentence, about your school function, your family's flag hoisting or your city, always performs better than a borrowed paragraph. Teachers can turn any line above into a writing prompt: ask students what the words ask of them this year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most famous Pakistan Day quote?

"Faith, Unity, Discipline," the famous guiding motto of Quaid-e-Azam Muhammad Ali Jinnah. It is quoted in almost every Pakistan Day speech and printed on banners across the country.

What is a good Pakistan Day slogan for a school function?

Short and chantable works best: "Pakistan Zindabad," "23 March: The Day a Dream Took Shape," or "One Nation, One Flag, One Promise." All three fit on banners and classroom charts.

Are these quotes and slogans free to use?

Yes. Jinnah's sayings are historic public remarks widely quoted for education, and the original lines and slogans on this page are free for personal, school and social media use.

What is a short caption for Pakistan Day 2027?

"Faith. Unity. Discipline. Happy Pakistan Day, 23 March 2027! 🇵🇰" Add one personal line about how you are celebrating for a caption that feels like your own.

When is Pakistan Day 2027?

Pakistan Day 2027 falls on Tuesday, March 23, 2027, a national public holiday marked by the Islamabad parade, flag hoisting and school ceremonies.

Conclusion

Words built this nation before borders did, and the right line still carries 23 March from the history book into the heart. Quote the Quaid with respect, borrow a fresh line or slogan from this page, and add one sentence of your own, because that sentence is your part in the story.
