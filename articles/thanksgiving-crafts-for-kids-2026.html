15 Easy Thanksgiving Crafts for Kids 2026: Fun Fall Projects

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Keep little hands busy while the turkey roasts. These fifteen Thanksgiving crafts for kids range from toddler-simple to tween-worthy, use supplies you probably already own, and double as table decor and grandparent gifts. Plan for two or three projects across the long weekend — enough to fill the afternoon without filling your house with glitter.

Toddler-Friendly Crafts (Ages 2–4)

At this age, process beats product. Keep it short, keep it washable, and embrace the mess.

  • Handprint turkey — the classic: brown paint handprint, googly eyes and a beak
  • Turkey feather gluing — glue paper feathers onto a paper plate turkey body
  • Pumpkin stamping — halved apples dipped in orange paint make perfect pumpkins
  • Leaf collage — gather real leaves and glue them into a fall crown or placemat

The All-Ages Handprint Turkey

The handprint turkey is the Thanksgiving craft every kid makes at least once, and for good reason: it is fast, it is adorable, and it produces a keepsake that goes straight to the fridge and then into a memory box. Trace a hand on construction paper, cut it out, glue on a beak, wattle and googly eyes, and write the child's name and the year.

Make it fresh in 2026: use patterned scrapbook paper for the tail feathers, add a family photo to the turkey's body, or make a wreath of handprints from every kid at the gathering. Grandparents will frame these. They will.

Paper Plate and Pumpkin Crafts

Paper plates are the secret hero of kids' Thanksgiving crafts — cheap, sturdy, and shaped like dinner. Turn one into a turkey face with a paper beak, a pilgrim hat with a black plate and buckle, or a pumpkin with orange paint and a green stem. Each takes about fifteen minutes plus drying time.

For the table: have kids make a paper-plate pumpkin for each place setting, with the guest's name written on it. It is a craft and a place card in one, which means the kids literally set the table.

Keepsake Projects for Grandparents

  • Thankful handprint wreath — every child's handprint traced, cut and glued into a wreath
  • Photo turkey — turkey body with a pocket holding the year's school photo
  • Gratitude tree leaves — paper leaves with each child's written thanks, tied to a branch
  • Painted mini pumpkins — small pumpkins decorated with acrylic paint pens, no carving
  • Turkey napkin rings — cardboard tubes wrapped in craft paper with feather fringe

Craft-Time Tips That Save Your Sanity

Cover the table with a plastic tablecloth or a flattened cardboard box — cleanup becomes a five-minute job. Put smocks or old t-shirts on kids before paint appears, not after. Pour paint into muffin tins instead of handing kids the bottles, and keep a damp towel at each seat for hands.

Work in batches: younger kids do the messiest crafts while the turkey is still in the oven and adults are distracted, then switch to stickers and coloring after dinner when everyone wants to sit still. And keep finished crafts somewhere visible — kids care more when their work is on display.

Displaying the Finished Crafts

Crafts stuffed in a drawer might as well not exist. Thanksgiving gives you a built-in gallery: tape finished projects to the fridge, string them along the mantel with twine and mini clothespins, or set up a kids' art table near the entryway where guests can admire the work. Photograph everything before the glitter sheds — a quick phone photo preserves the good ones forever.

Mail the best pieces to relatives who could not come. A handprint turkey in an envelope beats a store-bought card, and it costs a stamp. Keep one standout craft per child per year in a labeled folder; ten years of Thanksgiving art becomes a family archive worth more than any store-bought decoration.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the easiest Thanksgiving craft for toddlers?

Handprint turkeys and apple-stamp pumpkins — big motions, washable paint, instant results.

What crafts can kids do while dinner cooks?

Coloring, sticker scenes and leaf collages need no drying time and no adult hovering.

How do I keep craft time from getting messy?

Plastic tablecloth, muffin-tin paint cups, smocks before paint, and damp towels at every seat.

Which crafts double as Thanksgiving decor?

Thankful trees, paper-plate place settings and handprint wreaths go straight onto the table or mantel.

What can tweens make that doesn't feel babyish?

Painted mini pumpkins, photo turkeys and hand-lettered place cards feel grown-up and giftable.
