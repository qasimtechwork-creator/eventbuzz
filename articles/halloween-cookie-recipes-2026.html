Halloween Cookie Recipes 2026: Cutouts, Decorating & More

Published October 17, 2026 · Updated October 17, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team · 4 min read

Halloween cookies are the holiday’s most giftable treat: pretty, portable, and endlessly customisable. A batch of decorated sugar cookies works as party favours, dessert-table stars, and afternoon projects with kids.

These Halloween cookie recipes for 2026 cover cutouts, decorating, and no-bake options — for every skill level.

The Perfect Cutout Cookie

  • Dough: a no-spread sugar cookie recipe (with cornstarch) holds sharp shapes.
  • Chill twice: chill the dough, cut shapes, then chill cut cookies 15 minutes before baking. No blobs.
  • Shapes: pumpkins, ghosts, bats, hats, cats — nested cutter sets are worth it.
  • Bake cool: 175°C/350°F until just set; overbaking toughens them.

Decorating Like a Pro

  • Royal icing: flood consistency for bases, piping consistency for details. Practice on parchment first.
  • Colour strategy: mix black, orange, white, purple, green — a little goes far.
  • Beginner designs: ghost faces (two dots + oval), pumpkin lines, bat silhouettes.
  • Shortcuts: edible markers draw faces on plain iced cookies — kid-perfect.
  • Drying: let iced cookies dry 8+ hours uncovered before stacking.

No-Bake & Easy Options

  • Spider cookies: chocolate cookie + malt-ball body + pretzel legs. Adorable.
  • Mummy pretzels: white-chocolate-dipped rods with candy eyes.
  • Witch-hat cookies: cookie + kiss + frosting. Three ingredients.
  • Monster rice treats: coloured crispy treats with candy eyes. Kids’ favourite to make.

Gifting & Party Tips

  • Cellophane bags with ribbon turn cookies into favours.
  • Cookie-decorating party: bake ahead, let guests decorate — activity and dessert in one.
  • Mailing: layer with parchment in rigid tins; ship early in the week.
  • Labelling: tent cards with cookie names double as table decor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep sugar cookies from spreading?

Use a no-spread recipe (with cornstarch, no leavening overload), chill cut shapes before baking, and don’t over-cream the butter.

What is the easiest Halloween cookie to decorate?

Ghosts: white iced rounds with two chocolate-chip eyes and an oval mouth. Three dots, done.

Can kids help decorate Halloween cookies?

Absolutely — set out iced cookies, edible markers, and sprinkles. It’s the perfect low-mess decorating activity.

How far ahead can I make Halloween cookies?

Baked undecorated: freeze up to a month. Decorated with royal icing: 1–2 weeks airtight at room temperature.

Conclusion

Flour, sugar, and a little orange food colour — Halloween cookies prove the simplest treats make the sweetest memories. Bake a batch, share them widely.
