25 Christmas Dessert Recipes 2026: Sweet Treats for the Holidays

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Christmas dessert is the grand finale of the holiday meal, and the good news is that it is the easiest course to plan. Almost every great holiday dessert can be made a day or two ahead, which means dessert day is really dessert week, spread out in calm, enjoyable batches. From showstopper cakes to simple cookies, here are 25 Christmas dessert ideas for 2026, organized so you can pick the perfect ending for your feast.

Showstopper Cakes

If you want one dessert that makes guests gasp, bake a yule log (bûche de Noël): a chocolate sponge rolled with cream, frosted with ganache, and textured with a fork to look like bark. Dust it with powdered sugar snow and add meringue mushrooms for the full effect. A red velvet layer cake with cream cheese frosting is a natural Christmas choice, and a gingerbread layer cake with molasses and warm spices tastes like the holiday in cake form.

For chocolate lovers, a flourless chocolate torte is dense, elegant, and naturally gluten-free, while a cranberry orange bundt cake with a simple glaze is festive without fussy decorating. Cakes can be baked a day ahead and frosted the morning of serving.

Trifles, Puddings, and Creamy Desserts

Trifles are the smartest party dessert: no slicing, no decorating, just layers in a glass bowl. A chocolate peppermint trifle with brownies, chocolate pudding, and whipped cream is a 2026 favorite. Eggnog trifle with pound cake and spiced custard doubles as a way to use leftover eggnog, and a berry trifle with vanilla custard adds bright color to a heavy menu.

Spiced bread pudding with bourbon sauce is pure comfort and reheats beautifully. Panna cotta with cranberry compote looks restaurant-fancy but sets up in the fridge with five minutes of work, and chocolate mousse cups topped with crushed candy canes are portion-controlled elegance.

Classic Holiday Pies

Pie is the traditional Christmas dessert in many families. Pecan pie with its gooey brown-sugar filling is the holiday icon; add a splash of bourbon for depth. Apple pie with a lattice crust and a scoop of vanilla ice cream never disappoints, and pumpkin pie with freshly whipped cream bridges Thanksgiving nostalgia into Christmas.

For something different, try a chocolate silk pie with an Oreo crust, a cranberry curd tart with its jewel-red filling, or individual mini fruit galettes that let guests grab their own. Pies are best baked a day ahead so the filling fully sets.

Cookies, Bars, and Bite-Sized Treats

No Christmas is complete without cookies. Decorated sugar cookies are the holiday craft project and dessert in one. Gingerbread men, snickerdoodles with cinnamon sugar, Mexican wedding cookies dusted in powdered sugar, and peanut butter blossoms with Hershey kisses cover every craving. Peppermint brownies with a candy-cane crust and seven-layer magic bars are easy crowd-pleasers.

Put together a Christmas cookie platter with 4 to 5 varieties for the dessert table. Cookies freeze perfectly for up to 3 months, so bake them in early December and thaw the day before your party.

Dessert Planning Tips

Choose one signature dessert plus a cookie platter rather than five elaborate projects. Practical advice for a calm dessert season:

  • Bake ahead: Cookies freeze for months; pies and trifles are best made a day ahead; cakes hold 2 days under a dome.
  • Balance the menu: If dinner was heavy, choose a lighter dessert like panna cotta or fruit tart over a triple-chocolate cake.
  • Offer coffee and tea: A proper coffee and tea station with whipped cream, cinnamon sticks, and flavored syrups elevates any dessert.
  • Mind dietary needs: Include one gluten-free or dairy-free option, like flourless chocolate torte or coconut panna cotta, so everyone gets dessert.
  • Serve later: Wait 30 minutes after dinner before bringing out dessert so guests can actually enjoy it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most popular Christmas dessert?

Christmas cookies lead the pack, followed by yule log cake, pecan pie, and Christmas pudding in the UK. A cookie platter plus one signature dessert covers all bases.

What desserts can I make ahead for Christmas?

Cookies freeze for up to 3 months, pies and trifles are best made a day ahead, and cakes hold 2 days. Only whipped cream toppings should be done last-minute.

How do I keep a yule log from cracking?

Roll the warm sponge in a sugared towel immediately after baking, let it cool rolled up, then unroll, fill, and re-roll. The cake "remembers" the rolled shape.

What is a good gluten-free Christmas dessert?

Flourless chocolate torte, coconut panna cotta, meringue cookies, and flourless peanut butter blossoms are all naturally gluten-free crowd-pleasers.

How many desserts should I serve at Christmas dinner?

One signature dessert plus a cookie platter is plenty. More than two elaborate desserts usually means leftovers nobody touches.
