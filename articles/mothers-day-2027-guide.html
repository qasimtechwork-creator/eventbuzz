Mother's Day 2027 Guide: Gifts, Brunch & Celebration Ideas

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 3 min read

Mother's Day 2027 falls on Sunday, May 9, 2027. It is the one day of the year dedicated entirely to the women who raised us, and it deserves more than a last-minute card grabbed on Saturday night. Whether your mom loves a quiet brunch, a day out or simply time together, this guide has gift ideas, brunch plans and celebration ideas to make her feel truly appreciated.

When Is Mother's Day 2027?

In the United States and many other countries, Mother's Day is celebrated on the second Sunday of May, which lands on May 9 in 2027. The holiday traces back to Anna Jarvis, who campaigned for a day honoring mothers after her own mother's death; President Woodrow Wilson proclaimed it a national holiday in 1914. Note that dates differ elsewhere: the United Kingdom celebrates Mothering Sunday in March, and several countries mark the day on other dates. If your family spans borders, check both calendars.

Thoughtful Gift Ideas

The best gifts show you were paying attention. Jewelry is a classic for a reason: a pearl set, a pendant necklace or a personalized piece with initials carries meaning she will wear for years. Experience gifts often beat objects: a spa afternoon, concert tickets or a cooking class you take together. A framed photo, a photo book of the year, or a handwritten letter telling her what she means to you can outshine anything expensive. Flowers remain unbeatable when paired with that letter. And if you are truly stuck, ask her: many moms would rather choose than receive a guess.

Planning the Perfect Brunch

Brunch is the signature Mother's Day meal, and it works at home or out. Restaurants book up weeks ahead for the second Sunday of May, so reserve early if you are going out. At home, keep the menu relaxed but special: quiche or frittata, fresh pastries, a fruit platter and good coffee, with mimosas or a sparkling elderflower drink for a toast. Set the table with flowers and real napkins, and let her sit down while everyone else cooks and cleans. Breakfast in bed is lovely too, as long as someone else handles the dishes.

Ways to Celebrate Together

Time is the real gift. Plan a day trip to somewhere she loves, a walk through a botanical garden in full May bloom, or a simple afternoon of cooking her favorite recipes together. For moms far away, schedule a long video call, send flowers to arrive on the day, and consider watching a movie "together" over the phone. Grandmothers, stepmothers and mother figures deserve the same thought. Whatever you do, say it out loud: the words "thank you" land harder than any gift box.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Mother's Day 2027?

Mother's Day 2027 falls on Sunday, May 9, 2027, the second Sunday of May in the United States and many other countries.

Who started Mother's Day?

Anna Jarvis campaigned for a day honoring mothers in the early 1900s, and President Woodrow Wilson proclaimed the second Sunday in May a national holiday in 1914.

What is a good last-minute Mother's Day gift?

Fresh flowers with a heartfelt handwritten letter, breakfast in bed, or a gift card for an experience like a spa visit or dinner out all work beautifully at short notice.

Is Mother's Day celebrated on the same date everywhere?

No. The UK celebrates Mothering Sunday in March, and dates vary across the world, so check the local calendar if your family spans countries.
