Dhanteras 2026: Date, What to Buy, Puja Guide & Wishes

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Dhanteras opens the five days of Diwali with a prayer for the two greatest wealths of all: health and prosperity. Shops glitter, families bring home something new and auspicious, and the first diyas of the season are lit against the dark. This guide gives you the Dhanteras 2026 date, the story behind the day, what people traditionally buy, a simple home puja you can do in twenty minutes, and wishes to start the festival warmly.

Quick answer: Dhanteras 2026 falls on Friday, November 6, 2026, two days before Diwali on Sunday, November 8. Families worship Goddess Lakshmi, Lord Kubera and Lord Dhanvantari, traditionally buy gold, silver or steel for the home, and light the first diyas of Diwali. Confirm exact puja timings locally, as tithi windows vary by city.

What Is Dhanteras?

The name joins two Sanskrit words: "Dhan", meaning wealth, and "Teras", the thirteenth day of the lunar fortnight on which it falls. Two beloved stories shape the day. In one, Lord Dhanvantari, the divine physician, emerged from the churning of the ocean carrying the pot of amrit, the nectar of immortality — which is why Dhanteras is also a prayer for health and freedom from illness. In the other, a young prince was saved from a foretold death on this night when his wife filled the doorway with gold, silver and lamps, dazzling the god of death himself. Lamps and new metal at the door, then, are not shopping habits; they are the story retold every year. See where Dhanteras sits in the full festival in our Diwali 2026 calendar guide.

What to Buy on Dhanteras

Buying something new for the home is the day's signature ritual, symbolising prosperity entering the house. Traditional favourites, at every budget:

  • Gold or silver: A small coin with Lakshmi and Ganesha, a piece of jewellery, or silverware for the puja thali.
  • Steel and brass utensils: The most classic purchase of all — a new steel pot, brass diya or serving set that gets used first at Diwali.
  • The Lakshmi-Ganesha idol pair: Many families bring home the new idols on Dhanteras and worship them through Diwali.
  • A broom: A humble but deeply symbolic buy, sweeping away poverty and stale energy before the festival.
  • For the home and business: New account books, a safe, tools of your trade, or the first plant of the season. The rule is simple: something useful, welcomed with gratitude.

Whatever you buy, bring it home before the evening puja, place it near the idols, and use it first on Diwali day.

Simple Dhanteras Puja Guide

  1. Prepare (afternoon): Clean the puja area, bathe, and set a clean cloth. Arrange idols or images of Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Kubera (many also include Lord Dhanvantari and Lord Ganesha), along with your new purchases.
  2. Set the thali: A lit diya, incense, flowers, kumkum, turmeric, rice, coins, sweets and a small kalash of water.
  3. Begin (evening, after sunset): Light the diya, offer flowers and apply tilak to the idols. Sprinkle water and rice as offerings.
  4. Offer and pray: Offer sweets and coins, then pray in your own words for health first, prosperity second — the order is the whole teaching of the day.
  5. Light the doorway: Place diyas at the entrance and around the home, including the traditional lamp for Yama at the door, and share prasad with everyone present.

Keep it as simple or as elaborate as your family tradition likes; sincerity, not scale, is the measure. The main Lakshmi Puja follows two evenings later, explained in our Diwali 2026 date and calendar guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Dhanteras in 2026?

Dhanteras falls on Friday, November 6, 2026, the first day of the five-day Diwali festival. Diwali and Lakshmi Puja follow on Sunday, November 8, 2026.

What should I buy on Dhanteras?

Traditionally something new and auspicious for the home: gold or silver coins or jewellery, steel or brass utensils, the Lakshmi-Ganesha idol pair, or even a simple broom. Any budget works; the welcoming of the new is the point.

Who is worshipped on Dhanteras?

Goddess Lakshmi for prosperity, Lord Kubera as keeper of wealth, and Lord Dhanvantari for health. Lord Ganesha is honoured first, as at the start of every puja.

What time is Dhanteras puja performed?

In the evening after sunset, during the auspicious window (muhurat) that changes by city and lunar timing each year. Check your local temple or family calendar for the exact 2026 window in your area.

Why is a broom bought on Dhanteras?

The broom symbolises sweeping poverty, negativity and stagnation out of the home before Lakshmi arrives. It is one of the day's oldest and most democratic traditions: every household can afford the blessing.

Conclusion

Dhanteras asks for two prayers in the right order: health, then prosperity. Light the first diyas on Friday, November 6, welcome something new into your home, and the five brightest days of the year are underway.
