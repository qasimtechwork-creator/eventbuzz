Christmas Brunch Ideas 2026: Delicious Morning Recipes

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 5 min read

Christmas morning has its own rhythm: torn wrapping paper, new toys, and eventually, hunger. A proper Christmas brunch bridges the gap between present-opening and the main holiday dinner, giving everyone a reason to linger at the table together. The secret to a great holiday brunch is doing most of the work the night before, so you can actually enjoy the morning. Here are the best ideas for 2026.

Make-Ahead Brunch Favorites

Overnight dishes are the backbone of a stress-free Christmas brunch. An overnight French toast bake or a savory breakfast strata assembles in minutes the night before and goes straight into the oven Christmas morning. Cinnamon roll dough can rise slowly in the fridge overnight for warm rolls with minimal morning effort. These dishes all share the same advantage: while they bake, you get to watch the kids with their new toys instead of standing at the stove.

Sweet Treats

A holiday brunch table needs something sweet. Buttermilk pancakes with a cranberry-orange compote feel festive without much extra work. A simple coffee cake or a batch of gingerbread muffins keeps the kitchen smelling like December. If you want a showstopper, a pavlova topped with winter berries looks spectacular and can be made a day ahead. Set out maple syrup, whipped cream and a bowl of berries so everyone builds their plate their own way.

Savory Plates

Balance the sweets with something hearty. A frittata packed with spinach, cheese and roasted peppers feeds a crowd from one pan. Crispy hash browns or roasted breakfast potatoes are universally loved and easy in large batches. Smoked salmon on toast with cream cheese and dill feels special for very little effort. Keep the savory side simple and warm; the oven is already busy, and a sheet pan of potatoes roasts happily alongside almost anything.

Brunch Drinks and Coffee

Drinks make the morning feel like an occasion. A big pot of good coffee is non-negotiable, and a slow cooker of hot chocolate keeps kids happy for hours. Mimosas or a cranberry bellini add sparkle for the adults. Fresh orange juice in a pitcher, sparkling water with citrus slices, and a thermos of mulled cider round out the options. Set everything up as a self-serve station before the gift opening starts, so nobody has to ask.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time should Christmas brunch be?

Late morning, around 10 or 11, works best. It gives everyone time to wake up and open presents first, and it leaves a comfortable gap before the main holiday dinner in the afternoon or evening.

What can I make the night before Christmas?

Overnight French toast, breakfast strata, cinnamon roll dough, pancake batter, fruit compotes and coffee cake all work beautifully made ahead. Set the table the night before too; it saves real time in the morning.

How do I feed a crowd at Christmas brunch?

Choose dishes that scale in a single pan: sheet-pan potatoes, a large frittata, an overnight casserole and a self-serve drink station. Avoid anything that needs individual attention, like omelets to order.

What about dietary restrictions at brunch?

Offer one naturally flexible base, like a fruit spread or a potato dish, alongside clearly labeled options. Gluten-free and dairy-free guests are well served by fresh fruit, roasted potatoes and a simple egg dish.
