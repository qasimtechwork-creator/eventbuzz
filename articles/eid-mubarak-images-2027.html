Frequently Asked Questions

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid is expected on Tuesday, March 9 or Wednesday, March 10, 2027, confirmed by the sighting of the Shawwal moon on Chand Raat, the night before Eid.

Can I share these Eid images on WhatsApp and social media?

Yes. These images are shared for personal greetings, status updates and posts. Add your own caption and the name of the person you are sending it to for a warmer touch.

What makes a good Eid Mubarak image?

A clear festive symbol such as the crescent moon, a mosque, lanterns or an Eid table, warm colours, and uncluttered space where a greeting can be read at a glance.

Where can I find Eid wishes to go with the pictures?
