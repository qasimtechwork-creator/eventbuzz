Best Christmas Lights 2026: Outdoor Display Guide

By EventBuzz Team • October 19, 2026 • 7 min read

Nothing announces the holidays like a house glowing with Christmas lights. But with LED strings, net lights, icicles, projectors and smart systems to choose from, planning an outdoor display can feel overwhelming. This 2026 guide covers the best Christmas lights worth buying and a step-by-step plan for a display that delights the whole street — without tripping breakers or your budget.

Best Light Types for 2026

LED string lights: energy-efficient, cool to the touch, and the backbone of any display. Net lights: drape bushes and hedges in minutes for instant impact. Icicle lights: classic roofline magic. Laser/LED projectors: cover a whole house in minutes — perfect for beginners.

Planning Your Display Layout

Sketch your house front and mark focal points: roofline, windows, porch columns, trees and walkways. Start with the roofline and one hero feature, then fill in. Measure distances so you buy enough lights — most displays need more than expected. Map your outdoor outlets and plan extension cord routes before you climb a ladder.

Smart Lights and Timers

Smart plugs and light timers are the unsung heroes of 2026 displays. Schedule on/off times from your phone, sync colors to music with smart strings, and never again tramp outside in the cold to unplug everything. Solar options work for pathways where outlets are scarce.

Safety and Energy Tips

Use only outdoor-rated lights and cords, keep connections off the ground and dry, and never overload a circuit — LEDs help hugely here, drawing a fraction of old incandescent power. Use plastic clips, never staples or nails, and always have a spotter when on a ladder.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many lights do I need for my house?

A modest roofline needs 100 to 200 lights; a full house display typically uses 500 to 1,500. Measure your roofline in feet and buy strings accordingly.

Are LED Christmas lights better?

Yes — LEDs use up to 90% less energy, stay cool, last for years, and let you run far more lights on one circuit.

When should I put up outdoor Christmas lights?

Most people install them the weekend after Thanksgiving. In 2026, aim for late November so you enjoy them all December.

How do I keep my electric bill down?

Use LEDs, put lights on timers (6 to 10 hours nightly), and consider solar pathway lights for accents.

Conclusion

Start simple, plan on paper, and let your display grow each year. With the right lights and a little planning, your home can be the one cars slow down to admire this Christmas.
