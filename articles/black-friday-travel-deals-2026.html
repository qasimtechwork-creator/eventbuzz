Black Friday Travel Deals 2026: Discounted Flights, Hotels and Packages

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Black Friday on November 27, 2026 is not just for gadgets and gifts; it is one of the best days of the year to book travel. Airlines, hotel chains, cruise lines, and booking sites all run Black Friday and Cyber Monday promotions with discounted fares, bonus loyalty points, and package deals for trips through 2027. The key difference from retail shopping: travel deals are about booking windows, not doorbusters, so knowing when to look matters more than refreshing at midnight.

Flight Deals

Airlines typically release Black Friday fares the week of Thanksgiving, with the best prices appearing from Wednesday through Cyber Monday, November 30. Expect domestic round trips discounted 20 to 40 percent, with short-haul routes sometimes dropping under $100 round trip. International fares see smaller percentage cuts but bigger dollar savings: $200 to $500 off long-haul routes to Europe, Asia, and the Caribbean.

Budget carriers run the flashiest promotions, sometimes advertising one-way fares under $50, but legacy airlines compete with discounted premium economy and business class seats. The sweet spot for booking is travel in January through March and late April through May, when demand is low and the discounts are deepest. Holiday-season travel is usually excluded or only lightly discounted, so set expectations accordingly.

Hotel Deals and Resort Packages

Hotel chains go big on Black Friday. Major brands typically offer 25 to 40 percent off prepaid rates for stays through mid-2027, and many add bonus loyalty points worth an extra 10 to 20 percent in value. All-inclusive resorts in Mexico and the Caribbean run some of their best promotions of the year, with nightly rates discounted 30 to 50 percent and extras like spa credits, room upgrades, and free airport transfers.

Vacation rental platforms and boutique hotels join in with sitewide promo codes. If you are planning a specific trip, compare the Black Friday prepaid rate against the flexible rate: prepaid is cheaper but non-refundable, so only commit if your dates are firm. Booking directly with the hotel usually earns loyalty points and elite benefits that third-party sites do not offer.

Cruises and Tour Packages

Cruise lines treat Black Friday as a major booking event. Expect 30 to 60 percent off published fares, reduced deposits, free beverage or Wi-Fi packages, and kids-sail-free offers on select sailings. The deals usually cover sailings through late 2027, so this is the moment to lock in a spring or summer cruise at a fraction of the brochure price.

Tour operators and travel agencies run package deals that bundle flights, hotels, and activities at 20 to 35 percent off. Group tours to popular destinations like Italy, Japan, and national parks often add early-booking bonuses during Black Friday week. If you are celebrating a honeymoon or milestone trip, travel agents sometimes have access to consortium-only Black Friday rates that beat public pricing.

Luggage, Gear and Travel Accessories

Black Friday is also the cheapest time to upgrade your travel gear. Hard-shell suitcase sets from Samsonite, American Tourister, and Away typically drop 25 to 40 percent, with two-piece sets landing in the $120 to $200 range. Carry-on spinners, backpacks, and duffels see similar cuts.

Travel tech follows the broader electronics discounts: noise-canceling headphones, portable chargers, universal adapters, and e-readers all hit sale prices. Packing cubes, travel pillows, and toiletry organizers are inexpensive stocking stuffers at 30 to 50 percent off. If you are buying luggage as a gift, check the airline size restrictions first so the bag is actually usable.

How to Score the Best Travel Deals

Travel deals reward planners, not sprinters. Use this playbook:

  • Set fare alerts in October for the routes you want, so you recognize a real deal when Black Friday prices drop.
  • Be flexible on dates: shifting a trip by a week can double the discount, especially for international flights.
  • Book directly with airlines and hotels when the price matches third-party sites; you get better change policies and loyalty credit.
  • Read the fine print on travel deals: blackout dates, non-refundable terms, and excluded holiday periods are common.
  • Stack credit card travel portals: some cards add 5 to 10 percent back on travel booked through their portals during the holidays.
  • Check Cyber Monday too: many travel deals run through November 30, and some of the best hotel codes drop on Monday.

One caution: only book non-refundable travel deals when your plans are certain. A discounted fare you cannot use is not a deal at all. When in doubt, pay a little more for the flexible option, or wait for a sale with better terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Black Friday travel deals start in 2026?

Most airlines and hotel chains launch deals the week of Thanksgiving, with the best prices typically available from Wednesday, November 25 through Cyber Monday, November 30, 2026.

Are Black Friday flight deals refundable?

Many discounted fares are non-refundable or carry change fees. Always check the fare rules before booking, and consider travel insurance for expensive trips.

Do Black Friday travel deals include holiday dates?

Usually not. Most deals exclude peak holiday periods and target travel from January through May 2027. A few hotel chains include December dates, so read the terms.

Is it better to book travel on Black Friday or Cyber Monday?

Both. Airlines tend to peak on Black Friday weekend, while hotel chains and booking sites often save their best promo codes for Cyber Monday. Check both days.

Can I use loyalty points with Black Friday travel deals?

Sometimes. Many hotel chains let you earn points on discounted prepaid rates, and some run bonus-point promotions alongside the sale. Airline award availability is usually separate from cash deals.
