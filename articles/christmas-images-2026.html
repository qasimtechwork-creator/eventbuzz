What is the best Christmas image for WhatsApp?

A glowing tree or snowy night scene reads beautifully at small sizes. Add one personal line — even just the person's name — and it becomes a greeting instead of a forward.

Can I use these as phone wallpapers?

Yes. Save the image, then crop the centre when setting it as wallpaper. The tree-and-lights scenes keep their magic in a vertical crop because the main subject sits in the middle of the frame.
