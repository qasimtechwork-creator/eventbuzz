Christmas Movie Trivia 2026: 50 Questions for Holiday Game Night

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 8 min read

A Christmas movie trivia night is the easiest holiday party game ever: everyone already knows the movies, nobody needs to study, and the arguments about answers are half the fun. Below are 50 questions with answers, organized into five rounds of ten, from golden-age classics to modern favorites. Read them aloud round by round, keep score on a whiteboard, and crown your family's Christmas Movie Champion 2026.

Round 1: Timeless Classics

  1. In It's a Wonderful Life (1946), what does George Bailey's guardian angel Clarence earn by saving him? Answer: His wings ("Every time a bell rings, an angel gets his wings").
  2. What is the name of the town in It's a Wonderful Life? Answer: Bedford Falls.
  3. In Miracle on 34th Street (1947), at which department store does Kris Kringle work? Answer: Macy's.
  4. In the 1994 remake of Miracle on 34th Street, what is the fictional department store called? Answer: Cole's.
  5. In White Christmas (1954), what are the names of the two singing sisters? Answer: Betty and Judy Haynes.
  6. Which song does Bing Crosby sing in both Holiday Inn (1942) and White Christmas? Answer: "White Christmas."
  7. In A Christmas Story (1983), what gift does Ralphie want more than anything? Answer: A Red Ryder BB gun.
  8. What does Ralphie's father win in the newspaper contest in A Christmas Story? Answer: A leg lamp, "a major award."
  9. In Home Alone (1990), where is the McCallister family going on vacation? Answer: Paris, France.
  10. What are the names of the two burglars in Home Alone? Answer: Harry and Marv, the Wet Bandits.

Round 2: Modern Favorites

  1. In Elf (2003), what are the four main food groups of elves? Answer: Candy, candy canes, candy corn, and syrup.
  2. At which department store does Buddy get a job in Elf? Answer: Gimbels.
  3. In Love Actually (2003), what message does Mark show Juliet on his cue cards? Answer: That he loves her ("To me, you are perfect").
  4. Which actor plays Scott Calvin, the reluctant Santa, in The Santa Clause (1994)? Answer: Tim Allen.
  5. In The Santa Clause, what is the name of Scott Calvin's son? Answer: Charlie.
  6. Who plays the Grinch in the 2000 live-action How the Grinch Stole Christmas? Answer: Jim Carrey.
  7. What is the name of the Grinch's loyal dog? Answer: Max.
  8. In The Polar Express (2004), what is the first gift of Christmas? Answer: A bell from Santa's sleigh.
  9. In National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation (1989), how many lights does Clark hang on the house? Answer: 25,000 imported Italian twinkle lights.
  10. In Christmas Vacation, what does Cousin Eddie empty into the sewer? Answer: The RV's chemical toilet ("the shitter was full").

Round 3: Animated and Family Films

  1. In Rudolph the Red-Nosed Reindeer (1964), what is the name of Rudolph's girlfriend? Answer: Clarice.
  2. What does Hermey the elf want to be instead of making toys? Answer: A dentist.
  3. In Frosty the Snowman (1969), what brings Frosty to life? Answer: A magic silk hat.
  4. Who narrates the 1966 animated How the Grinch Stole Christmas? Answer: Boris Karloff.
  5. In A Charlie Brown Christmas (1965), what does Charlie Brown say his little tree needs? Answer: "It just needs a little love."
  6. What song does Schroeder play while the Peanuts kids dance? Answer: "Linus and Lucy."
  7. In The Nightmare Before Christmas (1993), who is the Pumpkin King? Answer: Jack Skellington.
  8. What town does Jack Skellington come from? Answer: Halloween Town.
  9. In Arthur Christmas (2011), what is Arthur's job at the North Pole? Answer: He answers the letters written to Santa.
  10. In Klaus (2019), what is Jesper's profession? Answer: A postman.

Round 4: Famous Quotes, Name the Movie

  1. "You'll shoot your eye out, kid!" Answer: A Christmas Story.
  2. "Merry Christmas, ya filthy animal!" Answer: Home Alone 2: Lost in New York.
  3. "Every time a bell rings, an angel gets his wings." Answer: It's a Wonderful Life.
  4. "The best way to spread Christmas cheer is singing loud for all to hear." Answer: Elf.
  5. "God bless us, every one!" Answer: A Christmas Carol (Tiny Tim).
  6. "I triple-dog-dare ya!" Answer: A Christmas Story.
  7. "Keep the change, ya filthy animal." Answer: Home Alone.
  8. "Seeing is believing, but sometimes the most real things in the world are the things we can't see." Answer: The Polar Express.
  9. "Maybe Christmas doesn't come from a store. Maybe Christmas, perhaps, means a little bit more." Answer: How the Grinch Stole Christmas.
  10. "Just remember, the true spirit of Christmas lies in your heart." Answer: The Polar Express.

Round 5: Behind the Scenes

  1. In what year was It's a Wonderful Life released? Answer: 1946.
  2. Which came first: Home Alone or The Santa Clause? Answer: Home Alone (1990; The Santa Clause is 1994).
  3. Who directed Elf (2003)? Answer: Jon Favreau.
  4. How old was Macaulay Culkin when he starred in Home Alone? Answer: 10.
  5. In which city is Die Hard (1988), the great Christmas action debate film, set? Answer: Los Angeles (Nakatomi Plaza).
  6. Which actor played Santa in all three Santa Clause films? Answer: Tim Allen.
  7. How many Home Alone movies feature Macaulay Culkin as Kevin? Answer: Two.
  8. A Christmas Story is set in the fictional town of Hohman, in which state? Answer: Indiana.
  9. Who wrote the 1843 novella A Christmas Carol? Answer: Charles Dickens.
  10. In The Holiday (2006), which two countries do the women swap homes between? Answer: England and the United States (Los Angeles).

How to Run Your Trivia Night

Split guests into teams of three to five and play the five rounds in order, with a short break after round three for snacks. Read each question twice and give teams thirty seconds to write their answer; one point per correct answer, with a bonus point in each round for the funniest wrong answer. For a tiebreaker, ask: "In what year was the first Home Alone released?" (1990) or have teams act out a Christmas movie scene for the room to guess. Prizes can be simple: candy canes for third place, a box of chocolates for second, and a silly trophy (a spray-painted dollar-store Santa) for the champions. Keep the mood light, let the debates run, and remember the host always gets to break ties.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many questions should a Christmas trivia night have?

Fifty questions in five rounds of ten is ideal for a two-hour evening with breaks. Fewer than thirty feels too short; more than sixty and guests lose energy.

What is a good tiebreaker for movie trivia?

Ask a year-based question like the release year of Home Alone (1990), or have tied teams act out a Christmas movie scene in thirty seconds for the room to guess.

How do you score Christmas movie trivia?

One point per correct answer, plus a bonus point per round for the funniest wrong answer. Tally on a whiteboard so everyone can see the standings.

Can kids play Christmas movie trivia?

Yes. Rounds 3 (animated films) and 4 (famous quotes) are the most kid-friendly. Let younger players team up with adults for the harder rounds.
