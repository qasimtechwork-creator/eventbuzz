Eid Mubarak Wishes 2027: Best Messages, Greetings & Quotes

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

A month of fasting ends with three beautiful words: Eid Mubarak. Whether you say them across the dinner table or send them across an ocean, the right wish carries the whole feeling of Ramadan with it, gratitude, relief and joy. Every message below was written fresh for Eid 2027 and sorted by reader, so you can greet everyone properly in minutes. Eid is expected on Tuesday, March 9 or Wednesday, March 10, 2027; our Eid al-Fitr 2027 date guide explains how the moon sighting confirms the day.

Quick answer: Short on time? Send: "Eid Mubarak! May Allah accept your fasts and prayers, and fill your home with sweetness, health and happiness today and always." Add the person's name and it is ready for anyone.

What to Write in an Eid Card

A memorable Eid card has four beats: the greeting, thanks for something shared in Ramadan, one dua for the year ahead, and a warm sign-off. Example: "Eid Mubarak, Ahmed! Thank you for the iftars that turned colleagues into friends. May Allah accept your fasts and open new doors for you this year. With warm wishes, Sara." Frequently Asked Questions

When is Eid al-Fitr 2027?

Eid al-Fitr 2027 is expected on Tuesday, March 9 or Wednesday, March 10, 2027, confirmed by the sighting of the Shawwal moon. Our date guide explains how the sighting works.

What is the best short Eid wish for WhatsApp?

"Eid Mubarak! May Allah accept your fasts and prayers, and fill your home with sweetness, health and happiness." It is warm, complete and fits in one message.

How do I wish Eid professionally to a client or boss?

Keep it gracious and brief: "Eid Mubarak! Wishing you and your family health, happiness and a well-earned celebration. Thank you for a wonderful year of working together."

When should Eid wishes be sent?

The first messages go out on Chand Raat, the night the moon is sighted, and continue through Eid morning after the Eid prayer. Close family and elders come first.

Can I use these Eid wishes on social media?

Yes. Every wish and quote here is original to EventBuzz and free to use in personal posts, captions, stories and cards.
