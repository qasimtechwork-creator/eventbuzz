Walmart Black Friday Deals 2026: Best Discounts to Watch

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 6 min read

Walmart is one of the heaviest hitters of Black Friday, and in 2026 it is expected to follow its established playbook: multiple deal events through November, online-first launches, and some of the lowest prices of the season on TVs, toys and small appliances. Black Friday itself falls on November 27 this year. Here is what to watch for, how Walmart structures its sale, and the strategy that gets you the best discounts without the 5 AM line.

How Walmart Structures Black Friday

Walmart typically runs its Black Friday as a series of events rather than a single day. Expect an early November kickoff event, a second wave mid-month, and the main event spanning Thanksgiving through the Black Friday weekend. Deals usually launch online first, often at 7 PM Eastern the evening before they hit stores, which means the website is the real front line. Walmart+ members have historically received early access windows, a few hours head start on the biggest online drops, which can be the difference between landing a doorbuster TV and watching it sell out. If you plan to shop Walmart seriously, the membership often pays for itself in a single Black Friday.

Categories With the Deepest Walmart Discounts

  • TVs: Walmart's signature category. Its onn. brand Roku TVs and name-brand 55 to 75-inch 4K sets routinely hit some of the lowest prices anywhere; check model numbers carefully on the cheapest units.
  • Toys: Expect doorbuster pricing on ride-ons, play kitchens, dolls and building sets, plus Walmart's own curated "top toys" list discounted early in November.
  • Small appliances: Air fryers, stand mixers, coffee makers and robot vacuums see steep cuts, often bundled with extra attachments.
  • Laptops and Chromebooks: Budget-friendly laptops for students and families get aggressive pricing, especially in the early events.
  • Smart home: Google Nest and Amazon Echo devices are perennial Walmart Black Friday staples at near-cost prices.
  • Video games: Console bundles and popular game titles get limited-time cuts, usually in the main Thanksgiving-week event.

Walmart+ Early Access: Is It Worth It?

For casual shoppers, probably not. For anyone targeting doorbuster TVs, consoles or hot toys, the early access window Walmart+ members get can be decisive, those items sell out in the first minutes of public access. Beyond Black Friday, the membership includes free shipping with no order minimum, free grocery delivery and fuel discounts, so weigh the year-round value too. A practical middle path: sign up for a trial or monthly plan in early November, use it through the Black Friday season, then decide whether to keep it. Just remember to set a calendar reminder before any trial converts.

Smart Walmart Shopping Strategy

Download the Walmart app now and save your payment and pickup details; checkout speed matters when doorbusters drop. Build your wishlist in the app and enable notifications for price drops on your saved items. For big items, choose free store pickup or delivery to avoid shipping delays, and check your local store's stock indicator before driving over. Walmart matches its own online prices in-store, so screenshot the online price as backup. Finally, watch for Walmart's "Black Friday Deals for Days" style weekly drops: some of the best prices of the season appear in the early November events, not on November 27 itself, so do not wait out of habit.

What to Watch Out For

As with any retailer, verify before you buy. Compare the doorbuster TV's exact model number against the standard version; stripped-down Black Friday variants are common. Check the return policy on electronics, which is often shorter during the holidays. And beware the inflated "was" price: use a price tracker or your own notes from October to confirm the discount is real. Walmart's rollback prices are usually genuine, but the loudest advertised savings deserve the closest look.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Walmart start Black Friday deals in 2026?

Walmart typically runs multiple Black Friday events through November, with deals launching online first, often at 7 PM Eastern the evening before in-store availability. The main event covers Thanksgiving week through the November 27 weekend.

Do Walmart+ members get Black Friday deals early?

Historically yes: Walmart+ members have received early access windows a few hours before the public on major online deal drops, which matters most for doorbuster TVs, consoles and hot toys.

What is Walmart usually cheapest for on Black Friday?

TVs (especially its onn. brand and name-brand 4K sets), toys, small kitchen appliances, budget laptops and smart home devices consistently see the deepest Walmart discounts.

Does Walmart price-match its own website in-store?

Yes. If you find a lower price on Walmart.com, stores will honor it at the register. Screenshot the online price as backup.

Are Walmart doorbuster TVs good quality?

The name-brand sets are generally fine, but check exact model numbers: some ultra-cheap doorbuster TVs are special variants with fewer features. Read the specs before buying.
