Classic Christmas Quotes (Public Domain)

These beloved lines come from books and poems that have been in the public domain for generations — free for anyone to quote, which is exactly what generations have done.

"I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year." — Charles Dickens, A Christmas Carol (1843)
"Happy Christmas to all, and to all a good night!" — Clement Clarke Moore, A Visit from St. Nicholas (1823)
"Christmas is the season for kindling the fire of hospitality in the hall, the genial flame of charity in the heart." — Washington Irving, The Sketch Book (1820)
"God bless us, every one!" — Charles Dickens, A Christmas Carol (1843)

Pair one classic line with one personal sentence of your own and the card reads both timeless and true. Classics carry the tradition; your sentence carries the love.

How to Pick the Right Quote for a Card

Match the quote to the relationship, not just the season. Grandparents and mentors deserve heartfelt or classic lines; close friends can take the funny ones; colleagues get short and warm, never edgy. One quote per card is enough — let it be the headline, then add one personal sentence underneath: a memory, a thank-you, a hope for their year. If you are writing many cards, pick three quotes (one classic, one heartfelt, one short) and rotate them; it keeps your hand from cramping and your words from going stale.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good short Christmas quote for a card?

Try "All is calm, all is cocoa" for something cosy, "Wrapped in gratitude this year" for something warm, or Dickens' classic "I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year" for a timeless card. Add the recipient's name above it and one personal line below.

What should I write under a Christmas photo on social media?

Short captions work best: "Merry, bright, and slightly overfed" for a feast photo, "Home is where the tree lights are" for a tree shot, or "Lights low, hearts full" for a family picture. Five to ten festive hashtags is plenty.

Can I use these Christmas quotes commercially or in cards I sell?

The Dickens, Moore, Irving, and King James Bible lines are public domain and free for anyone to use. The original EventBuzz one-liners are free for personal cards, posts, and messages; for commercial products, please credit EventBuzz or write to us first.

What is the most famous classic Christmas quote?

Dickens leads the list: "I will honour Christmas in my heart, and try to keep it all the year" and Tiny Tim's "God bless us, every one!" Clement Clarke Moore's "Happy Christmas to all, and to all a good night!" closes nearly every reading of his 1823 poem.

How do I choose between a funny and a heartfelt quote?

Ask what the person needs. Someone who had a hard year deserves heartfelt; someone who sends you memes daily can take the funny one. When in doubt, heartfelt in the card and funny in the follow-up message.

Conclusion

One good line can carry a whole card. Pick from the short, funny, heartfelt, classic, or religious groups above, add a name and one honest sentence, and Christmas 2026 is handled beautifully.
