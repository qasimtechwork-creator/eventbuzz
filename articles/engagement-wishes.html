Engagement Wishes & Congratulations Messages

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

An engagement is the happiest announcement there is — a question asked, a yes given, and a whole future suddenly on its way. Whether the news arrives as a ring photo in the group chat or a formal card in the post, your congratulations should match the size of the moment. Every wish below was written fresh for EventBuzz and sorted by relationship: friends, siblings, coworkers, and the ring ceremony itself. When the big day follows, our wedding wishes and messages will be waiting for you.

Quick answer: Congratulate, celebrate the couple, and wish the journey ahead well. Short on time? Send: "Congratulations on your engagement! The easiest yes in history, clearly. Wishing you both a lifetime that lives up to this beautiful beginning."

Engagement Wishes for Friends

Joyful and personal — for the friend whose happiness makes you tear up a little.

  • Congratulations, my friend! I have been waiting for this announcement, and that ring suits you almost as well as your fiancé does. So happy for you both.
  • You said yes to your favourite person and your best adventure. Congratulations — I am already emotional about the wedding.
  • To my dearest friend: may your engagement be the joyful first page of the best book ever written. Congratulations!
  • I knew it was forever the way you talked about them. Congratulations on making it official — let the celebrations begin.
  • Congratulations! Wishing you both a wedding countdown full of laughter and a marriage full of even more.
  • My friend found their forever person, and honestly, the rest of us never stood a chance against that smile. Congratulations!
  • From late-night talks about "the one" to an actual ring — what a journey. Congratulations, I could not love this more for you.

Engagement Wishes for Siblings

Part pride, part happy disbelief — for the brother or sister who is all grown up.

  • Congratulations, sibling! I shared a bathroom with you for eighteen years and someone still chose forever with you. True love is real. So proud of you.
  • To my brother: watching you become someone's whole future is the proudest plot twist of our family story. Congratulations!
  • To my sister: your fairytale just got its ring. Congratulations — may your fiancé always treat you like the treasure we have always known you are.
  • Congratulations! Our family just gained a wonderful new member, and I gained a front-row seat to your happily ever after.
  • From fighting over the TV remote to planning your wedding — look at us now. Congratulations, I love you and I love this news.
  • Congratulations, dear sibling. May your engagement be sweet, your wedding unforgettable, and your marriage the best story our family ever tells.

Engagement Wishes for Coworkers and Formal Contacts

  • Wonderful news! Warmest congratulations from all of us. May this exciting chapter bring you both great joy.

  • It is a pleasure to congratulate you on your engagement. Best wishes to you and your family for the celebrations ahead.

Ring Ceremony (Mangni) Wishes

In many families, the mangni — the ring ceremony where both families exchange blessings and the couple exchange rings — is a celebration of its own. These wishes honour that moment.

  • Mangni Mubarak! Two families, one beautiful promise. May this ring be the first of countless blessings for you both.
  • Congratulations on your ring ceremony! As these rings circle your fingers, may love circle your lives forever, without beginning or end.
  • Mangni ki lakh lakh badhaiyan! May the sweetness of today's mithai follow you into every day of your life together.
  • On your mangni, we pray your bond grows stronger with every ritual, every blessing and every elder's dua. Congratulations to both families!
  • The rings are exchanged, the families are one. Congratulations on a beautiful mangni — the wedding countdown officially begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best short engagement wish?

"Congratulations on your engagement! Wishing you both a lifetime that lives up to this beautiful beginning." It celebrates the news and blesses the future in two warm sentences.

What do you write in an engagement card?

Congratulate the couple by name, say one true thing about them ("You two make everyone believe in perfect timing"), wish the wedding and marriage well, and sign off warmly. Four short beats are plenty.

What is a mangni and how do I wish for it?

The mangni is the ring ceremony common in South Asian families, where the couple exchange rings and both families bless the match. Wish "Mangni Mubarak," congratulate both families, and bless the couple's journey towards the wedding.

When should engagement wishes be sent?

As soon as you hear the news — engagements are fresh, joyful announcements, and a same-day message means the most. A card can follow within the week if you want to send something handwritten.

Can I use these wishes for my message or post?

Yes. Every wish here is original to EventBuzz and free for personal use in cards, texts, captions and speeches. Adding the couple's names and the proposal story makes any line unforgettable.
