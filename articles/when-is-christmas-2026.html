When Is Christmas 2026: Date, Day & Fun Countdown Facts

Published October 10, 2026 · Updated October 10, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

The question lands in every home sometime in autumn: "How long until Christmas?" This page answers it properly — the date, the day of the week, the countdown from today, and the December dates that turn one holiday into a whole season. Save it, share it, and start the countdown.

Quick answer: Christmas Day 2026 falls on Friday, December 25, 2026. From October 10, 2026 — the day this guide was published — there are 76 days to go. Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, and Boxing Day follows on Saturday, December 26.

The Date at a Glance

  • Christmas Day: Friday, December 25, 2026
  • Christmas Eve: Thursday, December 24, 2026 — when many families exchange the first gifts and send their wishes
  • Boxing Day: Saturday, December 26, 2026 — a public holiday in the UK, Canada, Australia and many other countries
  • Advent begins: Sunday, November 29, 2026 — the traditional start of the Christmas countdown
  • New Year's Eve: Thursday, December 31, 2026, with New Year's Day on Friday, January 1, 2027

A Friday Christmas is a planner's gift: the holiday lands beside a weekend, so celebrations stretch naturally from Christmas Eve on Thursday through Boxing Day on Saturday and into Sunday.

Fun Countdown Facts Families Love to Share

  • 76 days separate October 10 and Christmas Day — that is 10 weeks and 6 days of anticipation.
  • There are 11 Saturdays left to shop, bake, wrap and decorate before the big day, counting from mid-October.
  • The countdown has an official warm-up act: Advent, the four-Sunday season of preparation that begins on November 29, 2026.
  • Children often count in "sleeps." From October 10, that is 76 sleeps — a number guaranteed to shrink faster than it feels.
  • Christmas 2026 is the second-to-last Friday of the year; the next Friday is New Year's Day itself.
  • The date is fixed but the day is not: Christmas moves one weekday forward each year, and two after a leap year. Next year it lands on a Saturday.

Why Is Christmas on December 25?

Christmas marks the birth of Jesus, and December 25 has been its date in the Western church since the 4th century. Historians still debate why that day was chosen — one theory links it to ancient winter solstice festivals of returning light, another to early church calculations of sacred dates. Whatever its origins, the date became universal through centuries of tradition, and the season around it grew layer by layer: Advent, Christmas Eve, the twelve days of Christmas, and Epiphany on January 6 for many traditions.

Key Dates for December 2026

  • Sunday, November 29: First Sunday of Advent — calendars open, candles start.
  • Tuesday, December 1: Advent calendars begin their first window (24 doors to go).
  • Sunday, December 6: St Nicholas Day, celebrated with small gifts in many European homes.
  • Thursday, December 24: Christmas Eve — services, family dinners and the great wrapping finale.
  • Friday, December 25: Christmas Day.
  • Saturday, December 26: Boxing Day — leftovers, sales and long walks.
  • Thursday, December 31: New Year's Eve. Start it with our New Year wishes.

How Families Count Down

Advent calendars are the classic tool — one small door per day from December 1. Paper chains work beautifully for young children: 25 links, one torn off each morning, the shrinking chain doing the math for them. Some families count down with kindness instead — one small good deed, one donation, or one phone call to a faraway relative per day. Frequently Asked Questions

When is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day falls on Friday, December 25, 2026. The date never changes; only the day of the week moves from year to year.

How many days are left until Christmas 2026?

From October 10, 2026, there are 76 days until Christmas Day. The count shrinks by one every morning — check any calendar and subtract, or count the Saturdays: 11 shopping Saturdays remain from mid-October.

What day of the week is Christmas Eve 2026?

Christmas Eve is Thursday, December 24, 2026. Many families hold their main celebration, gift exchange or midnight service that evening.

When does Advent start in 2026?

Advent begins on Sunday, November 29, 2026, four Sundays before Christmas Day. Advent calendars traditionally start on December 1.

Is Christmas Day a public holiday?

Yes, Christmas Day is a public holiday in most countries that celebrate it, including the United States, the UK, Canada and Australia, so schools, banks and government offices close. Boxing Day, December 26, is an additional holiday in many of those countries.
