Black Friday Furniture Deals 2026: Stylish Home Upgrades for Less

By EventBuzz Team • October 16, 2026 • 5 min read

Black Friday on November 27, 2026 is one of the best times of the year to buy furniture. Retailers discount sofas, beds, dining sets, and decor by 20 to 40 percent, and many waive delivery fees or offer white-glove setup for free. Because furniture is a considered purchase with long lead times, the smartest move is to start browsing in early November, shortlist your pieces, and buy when the Black Friday pricing lands.

Sofa and Living Room Deals

Living room furniture sees some of the strongest Black Friday discounts. Three-seat sofas and sectionals typically drop 25 to 40 percent, with popular midrange sectionals falling from $1,200 to the $700 to $900 range. Accent chairs, loveseats, and ottomans follow at 20 to 35 percent off, and coffee tables, TV stands, and bookshelves from big-box retailers land at some of their lowest prices of the year.

Online-first furniture brands run their own sitewide sales, often 20 to 30 percent off everything, which can beat the big-box stores on design-forward pieces. Look for free white-glove delivery offers, which normally cost $100 to $200, on sofas and sectionals. If you need seating for holiday guests, buy by mid-November at the latest so it arrives before Christmas.

Bedroom Furniture and Mattresses

Bedroom sets are a Black Friday sweet spot. Bed frames, dressers, and nightstand sets typically see 20 to 35 percent off, and bundle pricing on full bedroom sets can save several hundred dollars versus buying pieces separately. Upholstered headboards and platform beds from online retailers drop into very attractive territory during sitewide sales.

Mattresses deserve their own mention: nearly every major mattress brand runs a Black Friday sale with $200 to $500 off, free pillows, and free adjustable base upgrades. This is the single cheapest week of the year to buy a mattress. If you are pairing a new mattress with a bed frame, buying both from the same retailer often unlocks extra bundle savings.

Dining Room and Home Office

Dining sets get meaningful Black Friday markdowns, which is perfect timing ahead of holiday hosting. Five and seven-piece dining sets typically drop 20 to 35 percent, with solid-wood sets seeing the biggest dollar savings. Dining chairs sold in sets of two or four are frequently buy-more-save-more, and bar carts and buffets land at 25 to 40 percent off.

Home office furniture continues its strong discount run: desks, office chairs, and filing cabinets see 25 to 40 percent off at office supply and big-box stores. Ergonomic chairs from premium brands drop $100 to $300, making Black Friday the best time to upgrade a work-from-home setup. Standing desks and monitor arms round out the category with solid cuts.

Decor, Lighting and Outdoor Furniture

Small upgrades deliver outsized impact. Area rugs, often the priciest decor item in a room, drop 30 to 50 percent during Black Friday rug sales, with 8x10 rugs falling from $300 to under $180. Table and floor lamps, mirrors, wall art, and throw pillows see 25 to 40 percent off, making a full room refresh surprisingly affordable.

Outdoor furniture is the hidden gem: retailers clear patio sets, fire pits, and outdoor heaters at 40 to 60 percent off to make room for spring inventory. If you have storage space, buying now for next summer is one of the smartest moves in furniture shopping. Curtains, bedding sets, and seasonal decor round out the deals at 30 to 50 percent off.

Furniture Shopping Tips for Black Friday

Furniture has longer lead times and trickier logistics than electronics, so plan ahead:

  • Check delivery timelines before buying: made-to-order pieces can take 8 to 12 weeks, while in-stock items ship in days.
  • Measure doorways, stairwells, and the room itself; oversized sofas that cannot fit through the door are a costly mistake.
  • Compare the all-in price: delivery fees, assembly, and haul-away can add $150 to $300 at some retailers.
  • Read the return policy: furniture returns are often limited to 30 days and may carry restocking or return-shipping fees.
  • Look for floor models and open-box pieces in store for extra discounts on top of sale prices.
  • Shop early November sales too: many furniture retailers run the same prices all month with better stock.

One final tip: take photos and measurements of your room before you shop, and keep fabric swatches if you can get them. The best Black Friday furniture deal is the one that actually fits your space and your style.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Black Friday a good time to buy furniture?

Yes. Furniture sees 20 to 40 percent discounts during Black Friday week, with free or discounted white-glove delivery offers that are rare the rest of the year.

How much can I save on a sofa during Black Friday 2026?

Midrange sofas and sectionals typically drop 25 to 40 percent. A $1,200 sectional often lands in the $700 to $900 range, plus potential free delivery.

Will furniture arrive before Christmas if I buy on Black Friday?

In-stock items usually arrive within 1 to 3 weeks, but made-to-order pieces can take 8 to 12 weeks. Check the delivery estimate before buying if you need it for the holidays.

Are mattress deals better on Black Friday or other holidays?

Black Friday is the cheapest week of the year for mattresses, with $200 to $500 off plus free pillows and base upgrades. Presidents Day is the closest competitor.

Can I return furniture bought on Black Friday?

Policies vary widely. Many retailers allow 30-day returns but charge return shipping or restocking fees on large items. Read the terms before purchasing.
