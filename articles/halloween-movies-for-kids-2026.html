Halloween Movies for Kids 2026: Not-Too-Scary Family Picks

Published October 17, 2026 · Updated October 17, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team · 4 min read

Family movie night is Halloween’s cosiest tradition — but the line between “spooky fun” and “nightmare fuel” is different for every age. The right pick delights; the wrong one costs sleep.

These Halloween movies for kids for 2026 are organised by age, so every family finds its perfect not-too-scary feature.

Ages 2–5: Gentle & Sweet

  • Room on the Broom: the perfect toddler Halloween film — rhyming, kind, 25 minutes.
  • It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown: gentle, nostalgic, timeless.
  • Spookley the Square Pumpkin: the anti-bullying pumpkin tale.
  • Curious George: A Halloween Boo Fest: familiar friend, mild spookiness.

Keep it short, bright, and friendly — toddlers want pumpkins, not plots.

Ages 6–9: Spooky Fun

  • Coco: beautiful, moving, and the best Day of the Dead introduction.
  • Hotel Transylvania: monsters as comedy. Endlessly rewatchable.
  • ParaNorman: mild scares with a big heart — great for braver kids.
  • The Addams Family (animated): kooky, not creepy.
  • Hocus Pocus: the family Halloween classic. Some scenes may startle under-7s.

Ages 10–12: Thrills Without Trauma

  • Coraline: genuinely eerie but masterful — best with a parent nearby.
  • Goosebumps: monster fun at exactly the right level.
  • The Nightmare Before Christmas: the rare film that works for Halloween AND Christmas.
  • Ghostbusters (1984): the gateway horror-comedy. An all-timer.
  • Gremlins: rules, chaos, comedy — with parental guidance for sensitive kids.

Making Movie Night Magical

  • The setup: blankets, pillows, dimmed lights — see our movie night guide.
  • Themed snacks: popcorn “brains,” ghost bananas, pumpkin mandarins.
  • Intermission: pause halfway for a costume parade or stretch.
  • The debrief: talk about the scary parts after — naming fears shrinks them.
  • Morning after: a favourite funny film chaser resets any lingering spooks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Halloween movie for a 5-year-old?

Room on the Broom or It’s the Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown — gentle, short, and perfectly Halloween.

Is Hocus Pocus okay for kids?

Generally yes for 7+, though some scenes startle younger kids. Watch together the first time.

What if my child gets scared during the movie?

Pause, talk about it, remind them it’s pretend — and have a funny backup film ready. Never force finishing.

Are there non-animated Halloween movies for kids?

Yes — Hocus Pocus, Goosebumps, and Ghostbusters are all live-action and kid-appropriate with guidance.

Conclusion

The right Halloween movie makes the season for kids. Match the scare level to the child, keep the popcorn flowing, and enjoy the cosiest night of October.
