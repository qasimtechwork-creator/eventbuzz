Which Image Should You Send?

  • Family group: the family lighting diyas, or the classic greeting image with a personal wish added.
  • Friends: the fireworks night shot or the sweets platter with a funny caption.
  • Colleagues and clients: the diyas at dusk — elegant, calm and universally appropriate.
  • Status and stories: the rangoli top view or the decorated entrance; both crop beautifully on phones.
  • Dhanteras day: the gold and steel image, sent on November 6 before the main Diwali messages begin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these Diwali images free to use?

Yes, for personal use: greetings, WhatsApp statuses, stories, posts and cards. Please do not resell them or use them as your own commercial product artwork.

How do I download a Diwali image on my phone?

Press and hold the image, then choose Save Image or Download. On a computer, right-click and choose Save Image As. The saved file is full resolution and ready to share.

What is the best Diwali image for WhatsApp?

The classic greeting image with diyas and rangoli works best in chats because it reads clearly at small sizes. Add one line from our Happy Diwali Wishes hub to make it personal.

When should I send Diwali images in 2026?

Dhanteras images go out on Friday, November 6. Main Diwali greetings land best on the morning of Sunday, November 8, 2026, or during the evening puja for close family.

Can I use these as phone wallpapers?

Yes. Open the image, save it, and set it as your wallpaper from your gallery. The diyas-at-dusk and fireworks images look especially good on lock screens.
