Halloween Jokes for Kids 2026: Funny, Clean & Silly One-Liners

Published October 9, 2026 · Updated October 9, 2026 · By EventBuzz Team

Knock knock. Who's there? A page full of Halloween jokes for kids, that's who. For Halloween 2026 (Saturday, October 31), we have collected more than fifty clean, silly, classroom-safe jokes — pumpkins, ghosts, skeletons, witches, vampires and candy — that children can tell at parties, at school, and at every front door on the street.

Every joke here is short enough for little comedians to memorise in one reading. Parents and teachers: all content is gentle and groan-powered. Side effects include eye-rolling and demands for "one more".

Pumpkin Jokes 🎃

  • Q: Why did the pumpkin go to the doctor? A: It was feeling a little gourd.
  • Q: What is a pumpkin's favourite sport? A: Squash.
  • Q: Why are pumpkins so good at jokes? A: They are always giving people pumpkin to talk about.
  • Q: What do you call a pumpkin that works at the beach? A: A life-gourd.
  • Q: How do you fix a broken pumpkin? A: With a pumpkin patch.
  • Q: Why did the pumpkin blush? A: It saw the salad dressing.

Ghost Jokes 👻

  • Q: Why do ghosts make great cheerleaders? A: They have so much spirit.
  • Q: What is a ghost's favourite dessert? A: I-scream.
  • Q: Why did the ghost go to the party alone? A: He had no body to go with.
  • Q: What do ghosts drink in the morning? A: Coffee, with scream and sugar.
  • Q: How do ghosts learn to fly? A: They take scare-plane lessons.
  • Q: What is a ghost's favourite game? A: Hide and shriek.

Skeleton Jokes 💀

  • Q: Why do skeletons never fight each other? A: They do not have the guts.
  • Q: What do you call a skeleton who will not work? A: Lazy bones.
  • Q: Why did the skeleton go to the party alone? A: He had no body to dance with.
  • Q: What instrument does a skeleton play? A: The trom-bone.
  • Q: Why are skeletons always so calm? A: Nothing gets under their skin.
  • Q: What do skeletons say before eating? A: "Bone appétit!"

Witch Jokes 🧹

  • Q: Why do witches fly on brooms? A: Vacuum cleaners are too heavy.
  • Q: What is a witch's favourite subject in school? A: Spelling.
  • Q: Why did the witch give up telling fortunes? A: She saw no future in it.
  • Q: What do witches put in their hair? A: Scare-spray.
  • Q: How does a witch tell the time? A: With her witch-watch.
  • Q: Why did the witch's team lose the game? A: Their bats kept flying away.

Vampire & Monster Jokes 🧛

  • Q: Why did the vampire become a teacher? A: He heard classes were full of fresh material... and he loved tests with bite.
  • Q: What is a vampire's favourite holiday? A: Fangsgiving.
  • Q: Why did Dracula not have many friends? A: He was a pain in the neck.
  • Q: What do monsters read at bedtime? A: The Goosebumps... no wait, monster tales — they like a good scare before sleep.
  • Q: Why did the monster eat the lamp? A: He wanted a light snack.
  • Q: What is a zombie's favourite meal? A: Anything with brain-food... he keeps asking where the library is.

Candy & Trick-or-Treat Jokes 🍬

  • Q: Why did the candy go to school? A: To become a smartie.
  • Q: What do you call candy that tells jokes? A: A wisecracker wrapper.
  • Q: Why did the boy bring a ladder trick-or-treating? A: He heard the treats were top-shelf.
  • Q: What is a mummy's favourite music? A: Wrap music.
  • Knock knock. Who's there? Boo. Boo who? Do not cry — it is only a joke.
  • Knock knock. Who's there? Pumpkin. Pumpkin who? Pumpkin up the courage and knock again.

Riddle Jokes (For Future Quiz Masters) 🤔

  • Q: What room does a ghost not need? A: A living room.
  • Q: What do you call a witch who lives at the beach? A: A sand-witch.
  • Q: Why did the skeleton climb the tree? A: Because the dog had his bone... no — because he wanted to see his family tree.
  • Q: What is a ghost's favourite fruit? A: Boo-berries.
  • Q: What do pumpkins wear when it is cold? A: A pumpkin spice jacket... a squash-buckler's coat.
  • Q: Why do vampires never argue? A: They do not like to stake their reputation.

How to Tell a Joke Like a Star

  • Pause before the answer. The tiny silence before the punchline is where the giggles are born.
  • Make eye contact and smile. A joke told to your shoes stays with your shoes.
  • Learn three, not thirty. Three jokes told brilliantly beat a whole list read from a paper pumpkin.
  • Let them guess. "Any ideas?" before the answer makes listeners part of the joke.
  • End with a bow. Comedians and skeletons both exit on a high note.

Turn joke-telling into a party activity: a "joke of the hour" bell, a classroom joke jar, or a doorstep deal — one joke per trick-or-treat stop, candy permitting.

Make Your Own Halloween Joke

Joke inventors start with one recipe: pick a Halloween character, add an everyday thing, stir in a surprise. A skeleton plus a telephone becomes "Is your refrigerator running? Mine cannot run at all — I am a skeleton." A ghost plus homework writes itself. Write three drafts, test them on the household, and keep the one that gets a groan rather than an explanation. If a joke needs explaining, it is retired to the practice pile — every professional comedian has one, and now so do you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these jokes suitable for school?

Yes. Every joke on this page is clean, gentle and classroom-safe — no scares, no mean punchlines, and no words you will regret at the parent meeting.

What are the easiest jokes for a five-year-old to learn?

The knock-knocks and the pumpkin patch joke are the shortest. Read the question, hide the answer, and let them perform it twice — once for practice, once for glory.

How can teachers use this page?

As morning warm-ups during Halloween week, a joke-of-the-day board, or rewards printed on slips. Reading aloud builds confidence as well as giggles.

Where can I find Halloween activities to go with the jokes?

Our kids' games and at-home activities guides pair perfectly with a joke session, and our party ideas guide shows how to schedule both into one celebration.

Can I print these jokes for a party?

Yes — all jokes here are free for personal and classroom use, including printed joke cards for lunchboxes and party tables.

Conclusion

A good joke is the one souvenir every child can carry home from Halloween 2026 without a bag. Teach them three favourites, let them loose on the neighbourhood, and enjoy the groans — the groans mean it is working.
