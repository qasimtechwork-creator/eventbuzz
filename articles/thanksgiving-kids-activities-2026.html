Thanksgiving Kids Activities 2026: 25 Fun Ideas for Little Guests

By EventBuzz Team • October 15, 2026 • 6 min read

Thanksgiving is a long day for small attention spans. Between the morning wait and the slow dinner, kids need things to do — and the best Thanksgiving kids activities double as decorations, entertainment, and even helpful jobs. A little planning here pays off enormously: a craft table, an outdoor game, and a job in the kitchen can carry the whole day. These 25 ideas keep kids busy, happy, and part of the celebration.

Thanksgiving Crafts

Set up one craft table with supplies and let kids come and go all day:

  • Handprint turkeys: The classic — trace hands on construction paper, cut out, and decorate as turkeys with googly eyes and feathers.
  • Paper plate turkeys: Paint a paper plate brown, add construction-paper feathers, a beak, and wiggly eyes.
  • Thankful tree: A bare branch in a vase with paper leaves where kids write what they are thankful for and hang them up.
  • Pinecone turkeys: Glue feathers, googly eyes, and a beak onto pinecones collected on a nature walk.
  • Coloring pages: Print Thanksgiving coloring sheets and put out crayons and markers. Simple, quiet, and always popular.
  • Place card turkeys: Kids make the name cards for the dinner table — a craft that earns them a real job.

Active Games & Outdoor Play

  • Turkey tag: One player is the turkey, everyone else are farmers. A Thanksgiving twist on classic tag.
  • Pumpkin relay race: Teams race while balancing a small pumpkin on a spoon or rolling one with a broom.
  • Flag football: The traditional Thanksgiving backyard game for older kids and willing adults.
  • Leaf pile jumping: If the yard has leaves, rake a pile and let kids jump in. Nature's bounce house.
  • Scavenger hunt: Hide fall items (pinecones, acorns, mini pumpkins) around the house or yard with a simple picture list.
  • Corn toss: Toss beanbags or corn-filled socks into buckets at different distances for points.

Gratitude Activities

  • Gratitude jar: Everyone writes one thing they are thankful for on a slip of paper. Read them aloud at dessert.
  • Thankful ABCs: Go around the table naming something you are thankful for starting with each letter of the alphabet.
  • Gratitude placemats: Kids decorate paper placemats with drawings of what they are thankful for — then the family eats on them.
  • Thank-you cards: Kids write or draw thank-you notes to grandparents, teachers, or friends after the meal.

Kid-Friendly Cooking Jobs

Kids who help cook are kids who are not underfoot:

  • Washing produce: Even toddlers can rinse cranberries, potatoes, and apples in a big bowl of water.
  • Tearing bread: For stuffing, kids can tear day-old bread into cubes. Messy in the best way.
  • Mashing potatoes: With a hand masher (and supervision), this is a favorite job for ages 4 and up.
  • Stirring and pouring: Let kids stir batters, pour pre-measured ingredients, and sprinkle toppings.
  • Decorating desserts: Whipped cream dollops, sprinkle placement, and pie crust cutouts are all kid-safe decorating jobs.
  • Setting the table: Napkins, utensils, and the place cards they made earlier. A genuine contribution.

Surviving the Long Dinner

  • Quiet bags: Pack a small bag per child with coloring supplies, stickers, and a small toy for the table.
  • Kids' table: A separate table with a paper tablecloth they can draw on keeps them happily contained.
  • Serve kids first: Plate their food before the adults sit down. Hungry kids and long speeches do not mix.
  • Plan an exit: Let kids be excused after dessert to play or watch a movie while adults linger over coffee.
  • Movie wind-down: A family movie after dinner gives tired kids (and tired parents) a calm end to the day.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep kids entertained on Thanksgiving Day?

Layer activities through the day: a craft table for the morning, an outdoor game or nature walk before dinner, a job helping in the kitchen, and quiet bags or a movie for the evening. Rotating activities prevents the boredom meltdowns that come from one long stretch with nothing to do.

What are easy Thanksgiving crafts for toddlers?

Stick to simple, sturdy projects: handprint turkeys with washable paint, sticker decorating on paper pumpkins, and coloring pages. Avoid small pieces like beads for under-threes, and keep a smock or old shirt handy.

Should kids sit at the main Thanksgiving table?

Either works. A kids' table with a paper tablecloth they can draw on is often more fun for them and more relaxing for adults. If they sit at the main table, serve them first and let them be excused after dessert.

What can kids help cook for Thanksgiving?

Age-appropriate jobs include washing produce, tearing bread for stuffing, mashing potatoes, stirring batters, decorating desserts, and setting the table. Kids who contribute to the meal are more invested in it — and more likely to eat it.
